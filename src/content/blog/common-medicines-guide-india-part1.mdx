# Common Medicines Cheat Sheet - India Edition 🏥

:::note[📋 Important Medical Disclaimer]

This guide is for **educational and informational purposes only**. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

**Always:**
- ✅ Consult a qualified healthcare provider before taking any medication
- ✅ Follow prescribed dosages exactly as directed
- ✅ Inform your doctor about allergies and current medications
- ✅ Complete the full course of antibiotics (never stop midway)
- ✅ Read medicine package inserts carefully

**Never:**
- ❌ Self-prescribe antibiotics or prescription medicines
- ❌ Share prescription medicines with others
- ❌ Use expired medications
- ❌ Exceed recommended dosages

## <Highlight color='#8B0000' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🔴 GASTRIC / ACIDITY / ULCER MEDICINES </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#DC143C' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Proton Pump Inhibitors (PPIs) - Acid Reduction </Highlight>

**Pantocid Injection**
- **Salt/Composition:** Pantoprazole Sodium 40mg (IV injection)
- **Used For:**
  - Severe gastric acid-related conditions
  - GERD (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease)
  - Peptic ulcers (when oral intake not possible)
  - Zollinger-Ellison syndrome
  - Pre-operative prophylaxis (aspiration prevention)
- **How It Works:**
  - Proton Pump Inhibitor (PPI)
  - Blocks acid production in stomach
  - Reduces gastric acid secretion by 90%+
- **Route:** Intravenous (IV) - hospital/clinical use
- **Duration:** Single dose lasts 24 hours
- **When Given:** ICU patients, post-surgery, unable to take oral medicines
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹80 per vial
- **Note:** Prescription medicine, hospital/clinic administration

### <Highlight color='#DC143C' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Protective / Coating Medicines </Highlight>

**Sucrafil O Gel (Oral Suspension)**
- **Salt/Composition:**
  - Sucralfate 1g
  - Oxetacaine 20mg
- **Used For:**
  - **Peptic ulcers** (gastric/duodenal ulcers)
  - **Gastrointestinal ulcers**
  - Mouth ulcers (oral mucositis)
  - Esophageal ulcers
  - GERD with burning sensation
  - Stomach ulcer treatment and healing
- **How It Works:**
  - **Sucralfate:** Forms protective coating over ulcers, shields from acid
  - **Oxetacaine:** Local anesthetic - provides immediate pain relief
- **Dosage:** 1-2 teaspoons, 3-4 times daily (empty stomach, 1 hour before meals)
- **Best Time:** Between meals, at bedtime
- **Cost:** ₹120-₹180 per 200ml bottle
- **Note:** Do not eat/drink for 30 minutes after taking

---

**Digeraft Syrup (Oral Suspension)**
- **Salt/Composition:**
  - **Calcium Carbonate** 325mg (Antacid)
  - **Sodium Alginate** 500mg (Raft-forming agent)
  - **Sodium Bicarbonate** 213mg (Antacid)
- **Used For:**
  - **Heartburn**
  - **Acid reflux (GERD)**
  - Gastritis
  - Indigestion
  - Quick relief from stomach discomfort
  - Esophageal irritation
- **How It Works:**
  - **Calcium Carbonate & Sodium Bicarbonate:** Neutralize stomach acid rapidly
  - **Sodium Alginate:** Forms a protective "raft" barrier on top of stomach contents, preventing acid reflux into esophagus
  - Dual action: Neutralizes acid + Creates physical barrier
- **Dosage:** 1-2 teaspoons (5-10ml) after meals and at bedtime
- **Onset:** Works within 3-5 minutes
- **Cost:** ₹80-₹130 per 200ml
- **Important:** Shake well before use, take after meals for best results
- **Note:** Raft-forming action makes it particularly effective for acid reflux/GERD

## <Highlight color='#006400' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟢 ANTI-NAUSEA / VOMITING MEDICINES </Highlight>

**Emeset Injection**
- **Salt/Composition:** Ondansetron 4mg/2ml (IV/IM injection)
- **Used For:**
  - Severe nausea and vomiting
  - Chemotherapy-induced vomiting (CINV)
  - Post-operative nausea
  - Gastroenteritis with severe vomiting
- **How It Works:**
  - 5-HT3 receptor antagonist
  - Blocks serotonin receptors in brain (vomiting center)
  - Very effective antiemetic
- **Route:** IV or IM (intramuscular)
- **Onset:** 30 minutes
- **Duration:** 8-12 hours
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹50 per ampoule
- **Note:** Hospital/clinic use, very effective for persistent vomiting

---

**Buscogast Injection**
- **Salt/Composition:** Hyoscine Butylbromide 20mg/ml (antispasmodic)
- **Used For:**
  - Abdominal cramps and spasms
  - Intestinal colic (severe stomach pain)
  - Renal colic (kidney stone pain)
  - Biliary colic (gallbladder pain)
  - Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) spasms
  - Menstrual cramps (severe dysmenorrhea)
- **How It Works:**
  - Antispasmodic - relaxes smooth muscles in gut
  - Reduces spasms and cramping pain
  - Anticholinergic action
- **Route:** IV (intravenous) or IM (intramuscular)
- **Dosage:** 1 ampoule (20mg) as needed for pain
- **Onset:** 5-15 minutes (rapid relief)
- **Duration:** 4-6 hours
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹60 per ampoule
- **Important:** May cause dry mouth, blurred vision temporarily
- **Note:** Very effective for severe abdominal cramping, hospital/clinic use

:::info[Levocetirizine vs Cetirizine]
- **Levocetirizine** (3rd generation) is the active part of cetirizine, so it works at a lower dose (5mg ≈ 10mg cetirizine).
- **Both treat allergies** (sneezing, runny nose, itching) very well.
- **Levocetirizine** usually causes less drowsiness, so better for daytime use.
- **Cetirizine** may make you a bit more sleepy, so often given at night.
- **Both are safe and effective.**
:::

:::tip[Levocetirizine vs Cetirizine – Simple Analogy]
- **Levocetirizine** = just the sweet, juicy center of a rasagulla
- **Cetirizine** = the whole rasagulla
- **Levocetirizine** is a bit stronger and less likely to make you sleepy.
- **Use levocetirizine if you need to stay alert; cetirizine is fine for night.**
:::

## <Highlight color='#8B4513' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟤 ANTIBIOTICS (Prescription Only) </Highlight>

:::danger[⚠️ CRITICAL - Antibiotic Safety]

**ANTIBIOTICS ARE PRESCRIPTION-ONLY MEDICINES. NEVER SELF-PRESCRIBE.**

- ✅ Use ONLY when prescribed by a doctor
- ✅ Complete the FULL course (even if you feel better)
- ✅ Take at prescribed intervals
- ❌ NEVER share antibiotics
- ❌ NEVER stop midway (causes antibiotic resistance)

**Misuse of antibiotics causes:**
- Drug-resistant bacteria (superbugs)
- Treatment failures
- Serious health complications

:::

**Taxim O 200 Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Cefixime 200mg (3rd generation cephalosporin)
- **Used For:**
  - Urinary tract infections (UTI)
  - Respiratory infections (bronchitis, pneumonia)
  - Ear infections (otitis media)
  - Throat infections (pharyngitis, tonsillitis)
  - Typhoid fever
- **How It Works:** Broad-spectrum antibiotic - kills bacteria by disrupting cell wall
- **Dosage:** 200mg once or twice daily (5-7 days)
- **Age:** Children and adults (dose adjusted by weight)
- **Cost:** ₹150-₹220 per 10 tablets
- **Important:** Complete full course, take with or without food
- **Common Prescription:** Often prescribed along with nebulizer treatment (**Duolin 3 Respules + Budecort 0.5mg Respules**) for respiratory infections with breathing difficulty

---

**Cepodem 200 Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Cefpodoxime Proxetil 200mg (3rd generation cephalosporin)
- **Used For:**
  - Respiratory tract infections
  - UTI
  - Skin and soft tissue infections
  - ENT infections
- **How It Works:** Bactericidal antibiotic
- **Dosage:** 200mg twice daily (5-10 days)
- **Cost:** ₹200-₹280 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Take with food for better absorption
- **Common Prescription:** Often given with **Montek LC** (for respiratory infections with allergic component)

---

**Azee 250 Tablet (Azithromycin)**
- **Salt/Composition:** Azithromycin 250mg (macrolide antibiotic)
- **Used For:**
  - Respiratory infections (pneumonia, bronchitis)
  - Throat infections (strep throat)
  - Skin infections
  - Ear infections
  - Sexually transmitted infections (STIs)

- **Dosage:**

  - **Day 2-5:** 250mg (1 tablet) once daily
  - Total course: 5 days
- **Advantage:** Short course, once daily
- **Cost:** ₹100-₹150 per 6 tablets
- **Important:** Take on empty stomach (1 hour before or 2 hours after food)
- **Common Combination Prescription:** Often prescribed together with **D-Rise 60K** (Vitamin D), **Fucidin 15g** (topical antibiotic), **Linical Lotion** (soothing), and **Levocet 5mg** (antihistamine) for comprehensive infection treatment with skin manifestations

## <Highlight color='#8B0000' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> ⚠️ Important Safety Guidelines </Highlight>

**Always Read Medicine Labels:**
- ✅ Expiry date
- ✅ Dosage instructions
- ✅ Storage conditions
- ✅ Side effects
- ✅ Contraindications

**Storage Tips:**
- 🌡️ Room temperature (unless stated otherwise)
- 🚫 Away from direct sunlight
- 🔒 Out of reach of children
- 💧 Keep dry (avoid moisture)

**When to See Doctor Immediately:**
- 🚨 Severe allergic reactions (rash, swelling, breathing difficulty)
- 🚨 Worsening symptoms despite medication
- 🚨 Severe side effects
- 🚨 No improvement after 3-5 days

**Drug Interactions:**
- Inform doctor about ALL medicines you're taking
- Include supplements, herbal medicines
- Avoid alcohol with most medicines
