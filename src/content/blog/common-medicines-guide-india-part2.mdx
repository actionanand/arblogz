# Common Medicines Cheat Sheet - India Edition (Part 2) 🏥

:::note[📋 Important Medical Disclaimer]

This guide is for **educational and informational purposes only**. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

- ✅ Consult a qualified healthcare provider before taking any medication

## <Highlight color='#9B59B6' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟣 DIABETES MEDICINES (Anti-diabetic) </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#8E44AD' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Metformin-based Medicines </Highlight>

**Glyciphage 500mg Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Metformin Hydrochloride 500mg
- **Used For:**
  - **Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus**
  - Blood sugar control
  - PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)
  - Pre-diabetes (insulin resistance)
- **How It Works:**
  - Reduces glucose production in liver
  - Improves insulin sensitivity
  - Decreases sugar absorption in intestines
  - First-line medication for Type 2 diabetes
- **Dosage:** 
  - Starting: 500mg once or twice daily with meals
  - Maximum: 2000-2500mg per day (divided doses)
- **Best Time:** With or immediately after meals (reduces gastric side effects)
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹40 per 15 tablets
- **Important:**
  - May cause diarrhea initially (usually subsides)
  - Take with food to minimize stomach upset
  - Regular kidney function monitoring needed
- **Contraindications:** Severe kidney disease, liver failure, heart failure
- **Note:** Most commonly prescribed diabetes medicine worldwide

---

**Vildagard M 1000mg Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition (Combination):**
  - **Vildagliptin 50mg** (DPP-4 inhibitor)
  - **Metformin 1000mg** (Biguanide)
- **Used For:**
  - Type 2 Diabetes (when metformin alone insufficient)
  - Better blood sugar control
  - HbA1c reduction
- **How It Works:**
  - **Vildagliptin:** Increases insulin release, decreases glucagon (when blood sugar is high)
  - **Metformin:** Reduces liver glucose production, improves insulin sensitivity
  - Dual mechanism for comprehensive sugar control
- **Dosage:** 1 tablet twice daily (morning and evening with meals)
- **Cost:** ₹180-₹250 per 15 tablets
- **Advantages:**
  - Low risk of hypoglycemia (low blood sugar)
  - Weight neutral (doesn't cause weight gain)
  - Well tolerated
- **Important:** Monitor liver function periodically
- **Note:** Combination therapy for better diabetic control

---

**Glador 2 Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Glimepiride 2mg (Sulfonylurea)
- **Used For:**
  - Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
  - Stimulating insulin release from pancreas
  - When diet and exercise alone insufficient
- **How It Works:**
  - Stimulates pancreatic beta cells to release insulin
  - Reduces blood sugar by increasing insulin secretion
- **Dosage:** 
  - Starting: 1-2mg once daily with breakfast
  - Maximum: 6-8mg per day
- **Best Time:** 15-30 minutes before breakfast
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹60 per 15 tablets
- **Important:**
  - **Risk of hypoglycemia** - always eat on time after taking
  - Carry sugar/glucose candies for emergency
  - May cause weight gain
- **Side Effects:** Low blood sugar, dizziness, weight gain
- **Warning:** Never skip meals after taking this medicine
- **Note:** Older class of diabetes medicine, effective but requires careful monitoring

## <Highlight color='#FF8C00' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟠 LIVER & BILE HEALTH MEDICINES </Highlight>

**Bilypsa 4mg Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Saroglitazar 4mg (PPAR alpha/gamma agonist)
- **Used For:**
  - Diabetic dyslipidemia (high cholesterol with diabetes)
  - Hypertriglyceridemia (high triglycerides)
  - Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD)
  - Non-Alcoholic Steatohepatitis (NASH)
  - Insulin resistance
- **How It Works:**
  - Reduces triglycerides and cholesterol
  - Improves insulin sensitivity
  - Reduces liver fat accumulation
  - Lowers LDL cholesterol
  - Increases HDL cholesterol
- **Dosage:** 4mg once daily
- **Duration:** Long-term therapy as prescribed
- **Cost:** ₹150-₹220 per 10 tablets
- **Important:**
  - Prescription medicine for diabetic patients with high cholesterol/triglycerides
  - Regular lipid profile monitoring needed
  - Liver function tests required
- **Benefits:**
  - Comprehensive lipid control
  - Liver fat reduction
  - Well tolerated in diabetic patients
- **Note:** India's first indigenously developed drug for diabetic dyslipidemia

---

**Obetohep 10mg Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Obeticholic Acid 10mg (Bile acid analogue)
- **Used For:**
  - Primary Biliary Cholangitis (PBC)
  - Non-Alcoholic Steatohepatitis (NASH)
  - Chronic liver diseases
- **How It Works:**
  - FXR (Farnesoid X Receptor) agonist
  - Reduces bile acid production
  - Improves liver inflammation and fibrosis
  - Regulates bile acid metabolism
- **Dosage:** 5-10mg once daily (as prescribed)
- **Cost:** ₹800-₹1,200 per 10 tablets (expensive)
- **Important:**
  - Prescription medicine for specific liver conditions
  - Regular liver function monitoring required
  - May cause itching (pruritus)
- **Note:** Specialized medicine for advanced liver disease

---

**Livi-300 Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Ursodeoxycholic Acid (UDCA) 300mg (Bile acid)
- **Used For:**
  - Primary Biliary Cholangitis (PBC)
  - Gallstone dissolution (cholesterol stones)
  - Chronic liver disease
  - Primary sclerosing cholangitis
  - Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD)
  - Bile reflux gastritis
- **How It Works:**
  - Reduces cholesterol in bile
  - Dissolves cholesterol gallstones
  - Protects liver cells
  - Improves bile flow
  - Anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory effects
- **Dosage:** 300mg 2-3 times daily (or as prescribed)
- **Best Time:** With or after meals
- **Duration:** 6-24 months for gallstone dissolution
- **Cost:** ₹200-₹300 per 10 tablets
- **Important:** 
  - Long-term therapy often required
  - Regular ultrasound for gallstone monitoring
  - Effective for cholesterol stones (not pigment stones)
- **Note:** Gold standard for treating primary biliary cholangitis and dissolving gallstones

## <Highlight color='#DC143C' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🔴 GASTRIC / ACIDITY MEDICINES (PPIs) </Highlight>

**Rabimond 20mg Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Rabeprazole Sodium 20mg (Proton Pump Inhibitor)
- **Used For:**
  - GERD (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease)
  - Peptic ulcers (gastric/duodenal)
  - Erosive esophagitis
  - Zollinger-Ellison syndrome
  - H. pylori eradication (with antibiotics)
- **How It Works:**
  - PPI - blocks acid production in stomach
  - Reduces gastric acid by >90%
  - Allows ulcer healing
- **Dosage:** 20mg once daily before breakfast (can be 40mg for severe cases)
- **Best Time:** 30-60 minutes before first meal
- **Duration:** 4-8 weeks typically
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹110 per 10 tablets
- **Advantages:**
  - Fast acting
  - Long duration (24 hours)
  - Well tolerated
- **Note:** Similar to Pantoprazole/Omeprazole, different PPI class

---

**Pansy 40mg Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Pantoprazole 40mg (Proton Pump Inhibitor)
- **Used For:**
  - GERD, acid reflux
  - Peptic ulcers
  - Erosive esophagitis
  - Maintenance therapy for chronic acidity
- **How It Works:** PPI - reduces stomach acid production
- **Dosage:** 40mg once daily before breakfast
- **Cost:** ₹50-₹90 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Generic pantoprazole brand, same as Pantocid/Pantop

## <Highlight color='#8B4513' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟤 ANTIBIOTICS & ANTI-INFECTIVES </Highlight>

:::danger[⚠️ CRITICAL - Antibiotic Safety]

**ANTIBIOTICS ARE PRESCRIPTION-ONLY. NEVER SELF-PRESCRIBE.**

- ✅ Use ONLY when prescribed by a doctor
- ✅ Complete the FULL course
- ❌ NEVER share antibiotics
- ❌ NEVER stop midway

**Misuse causes antibiotic resistance (superbugs)**

:::

**Zerapod 200mg Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Cefpodoxime Proxetil 200mg (3rd generation cephalosporin)
- **Used For:**
  - Respiratory tract infections (bronchitis, pneumonia)
  - Urinary tract infections (UTI)
  - Skin and soft tissue infections
  - ENT infections (ear, nose, throat)
  - Gonorrhea
- **How It Works:** Broad-spectrum antibiotic - kills bacteria by disrupting cell wall
- **Dosage:** 200mg twice daily (morning and evening) for 5-10 days
- **Best Time:** With food (improves absorption)
- **Cost:** ₹150-₹230 per 10 tablets
- **Important:** 
  - Complete full course even if symptoms improve
  - Take with meals
- **Note:** Similar to Cepodem, effective against wide range of bacteria

---

**Nor-Metrogyl Plus Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition (Combination):**
  - **Ofloxacin 200mg** (Fluoroquinolone antibiotic)
  - **Ornidazole 500mg** (Antiprotozoal/Antibacterial)
- **Used For:**
  - **Diarrhea** (bacterial and parasitic)
  - Dysentery (bloody diarrhea)
  - Gastroenteritis
  - Intestinal infections
  - Mixed bacterial/parasitic infections
  - Traveler's diarrhea
  - Gynecological infections
  - Urinary tract infections
- **How It Works:**
  - **Ofloxacin:** Prevents bacterial cells from dividing and repairing by inhibiting DNA gyrase, thereby killing the bacteria
  - **Ornidazole:** Kills parasites and anaerobic bacteria (Giardia, Amoeba, Entamoeba) by damaging their DNA
  - Dual action for comprehensive bacterial and parasitic coverage
- **Dosage:** 1 tablet twice daily for 3-5 days
- **Best Time:** After meals
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹100 per 10 tablets
- **Important:**
  - Very effective for infectious diarrhea
  - Avoid alcohol (severe reaction with ornidazole)
  - Complete course even if diarrhea stops
  - May cause dizziness - avoid driving
- **Side Effects:** Metallic taste, nausea, dizziness, headache
- **Note:** First-line treatment for bacterial/parasitic diarrhea in India

## <Highlight color='#E74C3C' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🔴 PAIN RELIEVERS & ANTI-INFLAMMATORY </Highlight>

**Meftal 500 Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Mefenamic Acid 500mg (NSAID - Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug)
- **Used For:**
  - **Menstrual pain** (dysmenorrhea) - very effective
  - Headache, toothache
  - Muscle pain
  - Post-operative pain
  - Fever
  - Inflammatory conditions
- **How It Works:**
  - Blocks prostaglandin synthesis (pain and inflammation chemicals)
  - Reduces pain, inflammation, and fever
- **Dosage:** 
  - 500mg 2-3 times daily (maximum 3000mg/day)
  - Take for shortest duration needed
- **Best Time:** After meals (reduces gastric irritation)
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹80 per 10 tablets
- **Important:**
  - Do NOT take on empty stomach
  - Avoid in gastric ulcer patients
  - Use cautiously in kidney/liver disease
- **Side Effects:** Gastric irritation, nausea, diarrhea
- **Note:** Very popular for menstrual cramps in India

### <Highlight color='#C0392B' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Corticosteroids (Anti-inflammatory) </Highlight>

**Dexa Injection (Dexamethasone)**
- **Salt/Composition:** Dexamethasone Sodium Phosphate 4mg/ml or 8mg/ml (Corticosteroid)
- **Used For:**
  - Severe inflammation
  - Allergic reactions (anaphylaxis)
  - Asthma exacerbation
  - Cerebral edema (brain swelling)
  - COVID-19 (severe cases requiring oxygen)
  - Autoimmune conditions
  - Cancer chemotherapy (anti-nausea)
- **How It Works:**
  - Potent anti-inflammatory
  - Immunosuppressant
  - Reduces swelling, inflammation, allergic response
  - 25-30 times more potent than cortisol
- **Route:** IV (intravenous), IM (intramuscular)
- **Dosage:** Varies by condition (4-20mg per dose)
- **Onset:** Rapid (minutes to hours)
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹50 per ampoule
- **Important:**
  - Prescription medicine
  - Not for long-term use
  - May raise blood sugar
  - Weakens immune system temporarily
- **Side Effects:** Increased blood sugar, fluid retention, mood changes
- **Note:** Life-saving in severe inflammation/allergic emergencies

---

**Predmet 4 Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Methylprednisolone 4mg (Corticosteroid)
- **Used For:**
  - Inflammatory conditions
  - Allergic disorders
  - Rheumatoid arthritis
  - Asthma
  - Autoimmune diseases
  - Skin conditions (severe eczema, psoriasis)
- **How It Works:** Corticosteroid - suppresses inflammation and immune response
- **Dosage:** As prescribed (usually 4-48mg daily, depending on condition)
- **Duration:** Shortest effective period
- **Cost:** ₹80-₹140 per 10 tablets
- **Important:**
  - Take with food
  - Never stop suddenly (taper gradually)
  - May cause weight gain, fluid retention
  - Monitor blood sugar and blood pressure
- **Side Effects:** Increased appetite, insomnia, mood changes, weakened immunity
- **Note:** Potent anti-inflammatory for chronic conditions

## <Highlight color='#1E90FF' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 💙 CHOLESTEROL MEDICINE (Statins) </Highlight>

**Atchol-10 Tablet**
- **Salt/Composition:** Atorvastatin 10mg (HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor - Statin)
- **Used For:**
  - **High cholesterol** (hyperlipidemia)
  - High LDL ("bad" cholesterol)
  - Prevention of heart attack and stroke
  - Coronary artery disease
  - Diabetes with cardiovascular risk
- **How It Works:**
  - Blocks cholesterol production in liver
  - Reduces LDL cholesterol by 35-50%
  - Increases HDL ("good" cholesterol) slightly
  - Stabilizes arterial plaques
- **Dosage:** 
  - 10mg once daily (can be increased to 20-80mg)
  - Usually taken at night (cholesterol synthesis highest at night)
- **Best Time:** Evening/bedtime
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹80 per 10 tablets
- **Important:**
  - Long-term medication (lifelong in many cases)
  - Requires regular lipid profile monitoring
  - Liver function tests needed periodically
  - Avoid grapefruit juice (increases drug levels)
- **Side Effects:** Muscle pain, liver enzyme elevation (rare)
- **Lifestyle:** Must continue diet and exercise along with medication
- **Note:** Most commonly prescribed statin worldwide

## <Highlight color='#FF69B4' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🌸 SKIN / TOPICAL MEDICINES </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#FF1493' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Steroid Creams / Ointments </Highlight>

**Monovate-G Cream**
- **Salt/Composition (Triple Combination):**
  - **Mometasone Furoate 0.1%** (Potent corticosteroid)
  - **Gentamicin 0.1%** (Antibiotic)
  - **Miconazole 2%** (Antifungal)
- **Used For:**
  - Infected skin conditions (bacterial + fungal)
  - Eczema with secondary infection
  - Dermatitis (inflamed skin)
  - Fungal infections with inflammation
  - Mixed skin infections
- **How It Works:**
  - **Mometasone:** Reduces inflammation, itching, redness
  - **Gentamicin:** Kills bacteria
  - **Miconazole:** Kills fungi
- **Application:** Apply thin layer 1-2 times daily on affected area
- **Duration:** Usually 7-14 days (not beyond 2 weeks without doctor)
- **Cost:** ₹150-₹250 per 15g tube
- **Important:**
  - For external use only
  - Avoid face (unless prescribed)
  - Do not use on open wounds
  - Not for long-term use (skin thinning risk)
- **Note:** Comprehensive treatment for infected inflammatory skin conditions

---

**Derivate-GM Cream**
- **Salt/Composition:**
  - **Clobetasol Propionate 0.05%** (Very potent corticosteroid)
  - **Gentamicin 0.1%** (Antibiotic)
  - **Miconazole 2%** (Antifungal)
- **Used For:**
  - Severe inflammatory skin conditions with infection
  - Resistant eczema/dermatitis
  - Psoriasis with secondary infection
- **How It Works:** Same as Monovate-G but with more potent steroid (Clobetasol)
- **Application:** Thin layer once or twice daily
- **Duration:** Maximum 2 weeks
- **Cost:** ₹180-₹280 per 15g tube
- **Important:**
  - **Very potent steroid** - use strictly as prescribed
  - Never on face/genital area
  - Risk of skin atrophy with prolonged use
- **Note:** For severe skin conditions not responding to milder steroids

---

**Nuforce GM Cream**
- **Salt/Composition:**
  - **Clobetasol Propionate 0.05%**
  - **Gentamicin 0.1%**
  - **Clotrimazole 1%** (Antifungal)
- **Used For:** Severe skin inflammation with fungal/bacterial infection
- **Cost:** ₹160-₹240 per 15g tube
- **Note:** Similar to Derivate-GM (clotrimazole instead of miconazole)

---

**Smuth Cream**
- **Salt/Composition (Combination):**
  - **Lidocaine** (Local anesthetic)
  - **Zinc Oxide** (Astringent, skin protectant)
  - **Hydrocortisone** (Corticosteroid)
  - **Phenylephrine** (Vasoconstrictor)
- **Used For:**
  - **Hemorrhoids (Piles)** - internal and external
  - Anal fissures
  - Anal itching and discomfort
  - Swelling and inflammation in anal area
  - Post-surgical anal discomfort
- **How It Works:**
  - **Lidocaine:** Numbs the area, provides instant pain relief
  - **Zinc Oxide:** Protects skin, reduces moisture, promotes healing
  - **Hydrocortisone:** Reduces swelling, itching, and inflammation
  - **Phenylephrine:** Shrinks swollen blood vessels (hemorrhoids)
- **Application:** 
  - Clean and dry the affected area
  - Apply thin layer 2-3 times daily and after bowel movements
  - Can be applied internally (with applicator) or externally
- **Duration:** Usually 7-14 days
- **Cost:** ₹100-₹180 per 25g tube
- **Important:**
  - For external/rectal use only
  - Do not use for more than 7 days without doctor's advice
  - If bleeding persists, consult doctor
- **Advantages:**
  - Fast pain relief
  - Reduces swelling effectively
  - Multi-action formula
- **Note:** Effective for hemorrhoid pain, itching, and inflammation

  - **Physical Blockers:** Zinc Oxide, Titanium Dioxide
  - **Chemical Filters:** Avobenzone, Octinoxate
  - **SPF:** 30-50+ (Sun Protection Factor)

  - **Sun protection** (UVA and UVB rays)

- **Important:**
  - Essential for daily use (even indoors if near windows)
  - Use year-round

  - Controls oil

## <Highlight color='#B8860B' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟤 NERVE PAIN / NEUROPATHY MEDICINES </Highlight>

**Pregator Capsule**
- **Salt/Composition (Neuropathic Pain Formula):**
  - **Pregabalin** 75mg (Anticonvulsant/Neuropathic pain reliever)
  - **Alpha Lipoic Acid** 200mg (Antioxidant)
  - **Methylcobalamin** 1500mcg (Vitamin B12)
  - **Folic Acid** 1.5mg (Vitamin B9)
  - **Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine)** 3mg
- **Used For:**
  - **Neuropathic pain** (nerve pain)
  - Diabetic neuropathy (nerve damage in diabetes)
  - Post-herpetic neuralgia (shingles pain)
  - Peripheral neuropathy
  - Nerve damage from injury or disease
  - Fibromyalgia
  - Vitamin B deficiency-related nerve issues
- **How It Works:**
  - **Pregabalin:** Controls calcium channel activity in nerves, reduces pain signals to brain
  - **Alpha Lipoic Acid:** Powerful antioxidant - protects nerves from oxidative damage
  - **Methylcobalamin:** Active form of Vitamin B12 - repairs damaged nerve fibers and promotes nerve regeneration
  - **Folic Acid & B6:** Support nerve health and red blood cell formation
  - Combined action: Decreases pain while protecting and rejuvenating damaged nerve cells
- **Dosage:** 1 capsule once or twice daily (as prescribed)
- **Best Time:** After meals
- **Duration:** Usually 4-12 weeks or longer for chronic conditions
- **Cost:** ₹150-₹300 per 10 capsules
- **Important:**
  - May cause dizziness, drowsiness - avoid driving initially
  - Do not stop suddenly (taper gradually)
  - Avoid alcohol
  - Not for pregnancy/breastfeeding without doctor's advice
- **Side Effects:** Dizziness, drowsiness, weight gain, dry mouth
- **Benefits:**
  - Effective nerve pain relief
  - Nerve repair and protection
  - Reduces tingling, burning, numbness
- **Note:** Comprehensive neuropathic pain treatment with nerve regeneration support

## <Highlight color='#4682B4' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 💉 VACCINE </Highlight>

**Covishield Vaccine**
- **Salt/Composition:** 
  - ChAdOx1 nCoV-19 (Recombinant Adenovirus Vector Vaccine)
  - Contains weakened adenovirus carrying SARS-CoV-2 spike protein gene
- **Used For:**
  - **COVID-19 prevention**
  - Active immunization against SARS-CoV-2 virus
  - Reduces severity of COVID-19
- **How It Works:**
  - Viral vector vaccine
  - Adenovirus delivers COVID-19 spike protein gene to cells
  - Body produces spike protein
  - Immune system creates antibodies and memory cells
  - Protects against COVID-19 infection
- **Dosage Schedule:**
  - **Dose 1:** Initial dose
  - **Dose 2:** 12-16 weeks after first dose (optimal interval)
  - **Booster:** As per government guidelines
- **Route:** Intramuscular injection (upper arm)
- **Age:** 18 years and above
- **Efficacy:** 60-90% (depending on variant and interval)
- **Cost:** Free under government program; ₹600-₹1,200 at private centers (historically)
- **Side Effects (Common):**

## <Highlight color='#8B0000' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> ⚠️ Important Safety Guidelines - Part 2 </Highlight>

**Diabetes Medicines:**
- 🩸 Monitor blood sugar regularly
- ⏰ Take at fixed times
- 🍽️ Never skip meals after diabetes medicine
- 🍬 Carry glucose/sugar for emergencies (hypoglycemia)
- 📊 Regular HbA1c monitoring (every 3 months)

**Liver Medicines:**
- 🧪 Periodic liver function tests
- 🚫 Avoid alcohol completely
- 💊 Take full course as prescribed
- ⚕️ Regular doctor follow-ups

**Corticosteroids (Dexa, Predmet):**
- ⚠️ NEVER stop suddenly (taper gradually)
- 🩺 Monitor blood sugar and blood pressure
- 🦴 May weaken bones with long-term use
- 🦠 Increases infection risk
- 💊 Take with food

**Topical Steroids (Monovate-G, Derivate-GM):**
- ⏱️ Use for shortest duration (max 2 weeks)
- 🚫 Never on face (unless prescribed)
- 👶 Avoid in children (skin thinning risk)
- 📏 Apply thin layer only

**Antibiotics:**
- ✅ Complete full course
- 🚫 Never self-prescribe
- ⏰ Take at regular intervals
- 🍽️ Follow food instructions (with/without meals)

## <Highlight color='#4B0082' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Medicine Interactions & Contraindications </Highlight>

**Avoid These Combinations Without Doctor's Advice:**

⚠️ **Metformin (Glyciphage) + Alcohol** = Risk of lactic acidosis (dangerous)

⚠️ **Ornidazole (Nor-Metrogyl Plus) + Alcohol** = Severe nausea, vomiting

⚠️ **Atorvastatin (Atchol) + Grapefruit Juice** = Increased drug levels, muscle damage risk

⚠️ **Corticosteroids (Dexa/Predmet) + NSAIDs (Meftal)** = Increased gastric ulcer risk

⚠️ **Glimepiride (Glador 2) + Skipped Meals** = Severe hypoglycemia (dangerous)

⚠️ **Pregabalin (Pregator) + Alcohol** = Increased drowsiness, dizziness

⚠️ **High-dose Vitamin E (Pivens) + Blood Thinners** = Increased bleeding risk
