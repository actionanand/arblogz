# Common Medicines Cheat Sheet - India Edition (Part 3) 🏥

:::note[📋 Important Medical Disclaimer]

This guide is for **educational and informational purposes only**. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

**Always:**
- ✅ Consult a qualified healthcare provider before taking any medication
- ✅ Follow prescribed dosages exactly as directed
- ✅ Inform your doctor about allergies and current medications
- ✅ Never self-medicate with prescription medicines
- ✅ Read medicine package inserts carefully

**Never:**
- ❌ Self-prescribe antibiotics or prescription medicines
- ❌ Share prescription medicines with others
- ❌ Use expired medications
- ❌ Stop heart, BP, or mental-health medicines abruptly without doctor's advice

## <Highlight color='#9370DB' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟣 PAINKILLERS & NSAIDs (Analgesics) </Highlight>

:::caution[NSAID Safety]
Most painkillers below (except paracetamol) are **NSAIDs** (nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs) — take them **after food** to protect your stomach, and avoid long-term use without medical supervision.
:::

**Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)**
- **Salt/Composition:** Paracetamol 500mg / 650mg
- **Used For:** Fever, mild-to-moderate pain, headache
- **How It Works:** Acts centrally in the brain to reduce pain and fever; NOT anti-inflammatory
- **Dosage:** 500-650mg every 4-6 hours (max 3-4g/day)
- **Cost:** ₹10-₹30 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Safest painkiller for stomach; overdose harms the liver

---

**Ibuprofen**
- **Salt/Composition:** Ibuprofen 200mg / 400mg
- **Used For:** Muscle pain, pain with inflammation, mild fever
- **How It Works:** NSAID — blocks prostaglandins that cause pain and swelling
- **Dosage:** 200-400mg every 6-8 hours after food
- **Cost:** ₹15-₹40 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Avoid in ulcers, kidney disease, and asthma

---

**Diclofenac**
- **Salt/Composition:** Diclofenac Sodium/Potassium 50mg
- **Used For:** Joint pain, arthritis, back pain
- **How It Works:** Strong NSAID — reduces inflammation and pain
- **Dosage:** 50mg 2-3 times daily after food
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹50 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Use lowest effective dose; caution in heart disease

---

**Aspirin (Acetylsalicylic Acid)**
- **Salt/Composition:** Aspirin 75mg (low-dose) / 325mg (pain dose)
- **Used For:** Headache & fever (high dose); blood-thinning to prevent heart attack/stroke (low dose)
- **How It Works:** NSAID that also makes platelets less sticky
- **Dosage:** 75mg once daily (cardiac); 325-650mg for pain
- **Cost:** ₹10-₹30 per 14 tablets
- **Note:** Not for children (Reye's syndrome risk)

---

**Mefenamic Acid**
- **Salt/Composition:** Mefenamic Acid 250mg / 500mg
- **Used For:** Period (menstrual) pain, dysmenorrhea, mild fever
- **How It Works:** NSAID — relieves cramps and inflammation
- **Dosage:** 250-500mg 2-3 times daily after food
- **Cost:** ₹25-₹60 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Popular for menstrual pain relief

---

**Naproxen**
- **Salt/Composition:** Naproxen 250mg / 500mg
- **Used For:** Back pain, arthritis, long-lasting pain relief
- **How It Works:** Long-acting NSAID
- **Dosage:** 250-500mg twice daily after food
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹90 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Longer duration than ibuprofen

---

**Ketoprofen**
- **Salt/Composition:** Ketoprofen 50mg / 100mg (also gel)
- **Used For:** Sprains, strains, sports injuries
- **How It Works:** NSAID — anti-inflammatory and pain relief
- **Dosage:** As prescribed, after food
- **Cost:** ₹50-₹120 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Topical gel useful for localized sprain pain

---

**Piroxicam**
- **Salt/Composition:** Piroxicam 20mg
- **Used For:** Arthritis pain, chronic joint inflammation
- **How It Works:** Long-acting NSAID
- **Dosage:** 20mg once daily after food
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹70 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Once-daily dosing; higher GI risk with long-term use

---

**Nimesulide**
- **Salt/Composition:** Nimesulide 100mg
- **Used For:** Fever and pain, inflammation
- **How It Works:** NSAID
- **Dosage:** 100mg twice daily after food (short-term only)
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹50 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Not recommended for children; short-term use only (liver caution)

---

**Aceclofenac**
- **Salt/Composition:** Aceclofenac 100mg (often with Paracetamol)
- **Used For:** Pain and inflammation, muscle and joint pain
- **How It Works:** NSAID — well-tolerated anti-inflammatory
- **Dosage:** 100mg twice daily after food
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹90 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Commonly combined with paracetamol for stronger relief

---

**Tramadol**
- **Salt/Composition:** Tramadol 50mg / 100mg
- **Used For:** Moderate-to-severe pain (post-surgery, injury)
- **How It Works:** Opioid analgesic — acts on brain pain pathways
- **Dosage:** As strictly prescribed
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹80 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Prescription-only; can cause dependence, drowsiness, nausea

## <Highlight color='#C0392B' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🔴 BLOOD PRESSURE MEDICINES (Antihypertensives) </Highlight>

:::danger[Never Stop BP Medicines Suddenly]
Blood pressure medicines must be taken **regularly and long-term**. Stopping suddenly can cause a dangerous spike in blood pressure. Always adjust doses only under a doctor's guidance.
:::

**Amlodipine**
- **Salt/Composition:** Amlodipine 2.5mg / 5mg / 10mg
- **Used For:** High blood pressure, angina (chest pain)
- **How It Works:** Calcium channel blocker — relaxes and widens blood vessels
- **Dosage:** 2.5-10mg once daily
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹60 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** May cause ankle swelling; take at the same time daily

---

**Telmisartan**
- **Salt/Composition:** Telmisartan 20mg / 40mg / 80mg
- **Used For:** High blood pressure, heart & kidney protection
- **How It Works:** ARB (Angiotensin Receptor Blocker) — relaxes blood vessels
- **Dosage:** 20-80mg once daily
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹120 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Long-acting; good for diabetics with BP

---

**Losartan**
- **Salt/Composition:** Losartan Potassium 25mg / 50mg
- **Used For:** High blood pressure, kidney protection in diabetes
- **How It Works:** ARB — blocks vessel-narrowing hormone
- **Dosage:** 25-100mg once daily
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹90 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Often combined with hydrochlorothiazide (diuretic)

---

**Atenolol**
- **Salt/Composition:** Atenolol 25mg / 50mg
- **Used For:** High blood pressure and heart-rate control
- **How It Works:** Beta-blocker — slows heart rate, reduces workload
- **Dosage:** 25-50mg once daily
- **Cost:** ₹15-₹50 per 14 tablets
- **Note:** Avoid abrupt stop; caution in asthma

---

**Metoprolol**
- **Salt/Composition:** Metoprolol 25mg / 50mg
- **Used For:** Heart rate and BP control, after heart attack, palpitations
- **How It Works:** Beta-blocker — protects the heart
- **Dosage:** 25-100mg once or twice daily
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹80 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Do not stop suddenly (rebound risk)

---

## <Highlight color='#E74C3C' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> ❤️ HEART MEDICINES (Cardiac) </Highlight>

**Digoxin**
- **Salt/Composition:** Digoxin 0.25mg
- **Used For:** Heart failure, irregular heartbeat (atrial fibrillation)
- **How It Works:** Strengthens heart contractions, controls rhythm
- **Dosage:** As precisely prescribed (narrow safety margin)
- **Cost:** ₹15-₹40 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Requires monitoring; watch for nausea/visual changes (toxicity)

---

**Nitroglycerin (GTN)**
- **Salt/Composition:** Glyceryl Trinitrate 0.5mg (sublingual) / spray
- **Used For:** Acute chest pain (angina)
- **How It Works:** Widens coronary arteries, improves blood flow to heart
- **Dosage:** 1 tablet under the tongue during chest pain (repeat as advised)
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹100 per bottle
- **Note:** Sit down before use (can cause dizziness); seek help if pain persists

---

**Isosorbide Dinitrate**
- **Salt/Composition:** Isosorbide Dinitrate 5mg / 10mg
- **Used For:** Heart disease and angina prevention
- **How It Works:** Nitrate — relaxes and widens blood vessels
- **Dosage:** As prescribed
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹60 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Headache is common initially; do not stop abruptly

---

## <Highlight color='#2980B9' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🔵 DIURETICS (Water Pills) </Highlight>

**Furosemide**
- **Salt/Composition:** Furosemide 40mg
- **Used For:** Removing excess fluid (heart failure, swelling, high BP)
- **How It Works:** Loop diuretic — makes kidneys remove salt and water
- **Dosage:** 20-80mg once or twice daily (morning preferred)
- **Cost:** ₹10-₹40 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Take in the morning to avoid night-time urination; can lower potassium

---

**Spironolactone**
- **Salt/Composition:** Spironolactone 25mg / 50mg
- **Used For:** Swelling and fluid retention, heart failure, high BP
- **How It Works:** Potassium-sparing diuretic — removes water while keeping potassium
- **Dosage:** 25-50mg once daily
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹80 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Monitor potassium; also used for hormonal acne/PCOS

---

## <Highlight color='#E67E22' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟠 CHOLESTEROL MEDICINES (Statins) </Highlight>

**Atorvastatin**
- **Salt/Composition:** Atorvastatin 10mg / 20mg / 40mg
- **Used For:** Lowering cholesterol, preventing heart attack & stroke
- **How It Works:** Statin — blocks liver enzyme that makes cholesterol
- **Dosage:** 10-40mg once daily (night preferred)
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹120 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Report unexplained muscle pain to your doctor

---

**Rosuvastatin**
- **Salt/Composition:** Rosuvastatin 5mg / 10mg / 20mg
- **Used For:** Lowering cholesterol, cardiovascular protection
- **How It Works:** Potent statin — reduces LDL ("bad") cholesterol
- **Dosage:** 5-20mg once daily (any time)
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹150 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** More potent than atorvastatin at lower doses

## <Highlight color='#8E44AD' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟣 BLOOD THINNERS (Anticoagulants & Antiplatelets) </Highlight>

:::danger[Bleeding Risk]
Blood thinners increase bleeding risk. Watch for unusual bruising, black stools, or prolonged bleeding, and inform any doctor/dentist before procedures.
:::

**Clopidogrel**
- **Salt/Composition:** Clopidogrel 75mg
- **Used For:** Preventing blood clots after heart attack, stroke, or stent
- **How It Works:** Antiplatelet — stops platelets from clumping
- **Dosage:** 75mg once daily
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹120 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Often combined with aspirin (dual therapy)

---

**Warfarin**
- **Salt/Composition:** Warfarin 1mg / 2mg / 5mg
- **Used For:** Preventing/treating blood clots (DVT, atrial fibrillation, valves)
- **How It Works:** Anticoagulant — blocks vitamin K clotting factors
- **Dosage:** Individualized based on INR blood test
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹60 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Requires regular INR monitoring; watch diet (vitamin K foods)

---

**Heparin**
- **Salt/Composition:** Heparin Sodium (injection)
- **Used For:** Preventing blood clots (hospital/surgery, DVT)
- **How It Works:** Fast-acting injectable anticoagulant
- **Dosage:** As prescribed (IV/subcutaneous)
- **Cost:** Varies (hospital use)
- **Note:** Hospital/clinical use; monitored closely

---

## <Highlight color='#16A085' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟢 ANTIFUNGAL MEDICINES </Highlight>

**Clotrimazole**
- **Salt/Composition:** Clotrimazole 1% cream / powder
- **Used For:** Itching and fungal skin infections (ringworm, athlete's foot)
- **How It Works:** Antifungal — damages fungal cell membranes
- **Dosage:** Apply 2-3 times daily for 2-4 weeks
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹90 per tube
- **Note:** Continue a few days after symptoms clear to prevent relapse

---

**Miconazole**
- **Salt/Composition:** Miconazole 2% cream / gel
- **Used For:** Fungal infections of skin and mouth (oral thrush)
- **How It Works:** Antifungal — inhibits fungal growth
- **Dosage:** Apply 2 times daily
- **Cost:** ₹50-₹100 per tube
- **Note:** Oral gel available for mouth infections

---

**Ketoconazole**
- **Salt/Composition:** Ketoconazole 2% shampoo / cream
- **Used For:** Dandruff and fungal infections (seborrheic dermatitis)
- **How It Works:** Antifungal — clears fungus on skin/scalp
- **Dosage:** Shampoo twice weekly; cream once/twice daily
- **Cost:** ₹120-₹280 per bottle
- **Note:** Leave shampoo 3-5 minutes before rinsing

---

## <Highlight color='#795548' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟤 DEWORMING & ANTIPARASITIC MEDICINES </Highlight>

**Albendazole**
- **Salt/Composition:** Albendazole 400mg
- **Used For:** Intestinal worms (roundworm, hookworm, pinworm)
- **How It Works:** Anthelmintic — starves and kills worms
- **Dosage:** 400mg single dose (may repeat after 2 weeks)
- **Cost:** ₹15-₹40 per tablet
- **Note:** Chewable; deworm whole family together

---

**Mebendazole**
- **Salt/Composition:** Mebendazole 100mg
- **Used For:** Intestinal worms
- **How It Works:** Anthelmintic — blocks worms' sugar uptake
- **Dosage:** 100mg twice daily for 3 days (or as advised)
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹50 per pack
- **Note:** Effective for mixed worm infections

---

**Levamisole**
- **Salt/Composition:** Levamisole 50mg / 150mg
- **Used For:** Intestinal worms, especially in children
- **How It Works:** Paralyses worms so they are expelled
- **Dosage:** As prescribed by weight
- **Cost:** ₹15-₹40 per pack
- **Note:** Single-dose convenience for roundworm

---

**Diethylcarbamazine (DEC)**
- **Salt/Composition:** Diethylcarbamazine 100mg
- **Used For:** Filariasis (elephantiasis-causing parasite)
- **How It Works:** Kills microfilariae (parasitic worm larvae)
- **Dosage:** As prescribed (weight-based course)
- **Cost:** ₹15-₹40 per pack
- **Note:** Part of national filariasis elimination programs

---

**Ivermectin**
- **Salt/Composition:** Ivermectin 6mg / 12mg
- **Used For:** Scabies, lice, and parasitic worm infections
- **How It Works:** Paralyses and kills parasites
- **Dosage:** Single weight-based dose (repeat as advised)
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹80 per tablet
- **Note:** For scabies, treat close contacts and wash bedding

---

## <Highlight color='#D35400' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🟠 MUSCLE RELAXANTS </Highlight>

**Chlorzoxazone**
- **Salt/Composition:** Chlorzoxazone 250mg / 500mg
- **Used For:** Muscle pain and spasm
- **How It Works:** Central muscle relaxant — reduces muscle stiffness
- **Dosage:** As prescribed, after food
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹90 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Often combined with paracetamol/NSAID (e.g., "MR" tablets)

---

**Tizanidine**
- **Salt/Composition:** Tizanidine 2mg / 4mg
- **Used For:** Muscle tightness and spasm
- **How It Works:** Relaxes muscles via the nervous system
- **Dosage:** 2-4mg 2-3 times daily
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹80 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Can cause drowsiness and dry mouth

---

**Thiocolchicoside**
- **Salt/Composition:** Thiocolchicoside 4mg / 8mg
- **Used For:** Neck pain and muscle spasm, back stiffness
- **How It Works:** Muscle relaxant — relieves painful spasms
- **Dosage:** 4-8mg twice daily (short-term)
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹140 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Short-term use only

---

**Cyclobenzaprine**
- **Salt/Composition:** Cyclobenzaprine 5mg / 10mg
- **Used For:** Back pain and muscle spasm
- **How It Works:** Central muscle relaxant
- **Dosage:** 5-10mg up to 3 times daily
- **Cost:** ₹50-₹120 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Causes drowsiness; avoid driving

## <Highlight color='#2C3E50' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🧠 MENTAL HEALTH & NEURO-PSYCHIATRIC MEDICINES </Highlight>

:::danger[Do Not Self-Medicate]
These are **prescription-only** medicines. They must be started, adjusted, and stopped **only under a psychiatrist's/doctor's supervision**. Never stop suddenly — tapering is required.
:::

**Fluoxetine**
- **Salt/Composition:** Fluoxetine 10mg / 20mg
- **Used For:** Depression, OCD, anxiety
- **How It Works:** SSRI — increases serotonin in the brain
- **Dosage:** 20mg once daily (as prescribed)
- **Cost:** ₹30-₹80 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** May take 2-4 weeks to show benefit

---

**Sertraline**
- **Salt/Composition:** Sertraline 25mg / 50mg / 100mg
- **Used For:** Anxiety and depression, panic disorder, PTSD
- **How It Works:** SSRI — boosts serotonin
- **Dosage:** 25-100mg once daily
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹120 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Do not stop abruptly (withdrawal symptoms)

---

**Alprazolam**
- **Salt/Composition:** Alprazolam 0.25mg / 0.5mg
- **Used For:** Anxiety and sleep problems (short-term)
- **How It Works:** Benzodiazepine — calms the nervous system
- **Dosage:** Lowest effective dose, short-term only
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹60 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** High dependence risk; strictly controlled

---

**Diazepam**
- **Salt/Composition:** Diazepam 2mg / 5mg / 10mg
- **Used For:** Anxiety, muscle relaxant, seizures, alcohol withdrawal
- **How It Works:** Benzodiazepine — sedative and muscle relaxant
- **Dosage:** As strictly prescribed
- **Cost:** ₹15-₹50 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Causes drowsiness; dependence risk with long use

---

**Haloperidol**
- **Salt/Composition:** Haloperidol 0.5mg / 5mg
- **Used For:** Serious mental-health disorders (schizophrenia, psychosis, severe agitation)
- **How It Works:** Antipsychotic — balances dopamine in the brain
- **Dosage:** As prescribed by a psychiatrist
- **Cost:** ₹20-₹60 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Requires medical monitoring for movement side effects

---

## <Highlight color='#34495E' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🔷 MEN'S HEALTH & UROLOGY MEDICINES </Highlight>

**Sildenafil**
- **Salt/Composition:** Sildenafil 25mg / 50mg / 100mg
- **Used For:** Erectile dysfunction (also pulmonary hypertension)
- **How It Works:** PDE5 inhibitor — improves blood flow
- **Dosage:** As prescribed, ~1 hour before activity
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹150 per pack
- **Note:** Never combine with nitrates (dangerous BP drop)

---

**Tadalafil**
- **Salt/Composition:** Tadalafil 10mg / 20mg
- **Used For:** Erectile dysfunction, enlarged prostate (BPH)
- **How It Works:** Long-acting PDE5 inhibitor
- **Dosage:** As prescribed
- **Cost:** ₹50-₹180 per pack
- **Note:** Longer duration than sildenafil; avoid nitrates

---

**Dapoxetine**
- **Salt/Composition:** Dapoxetine 30mg / 60mg
- **Used For:** Premature ejaculation
- **How It Works:** Short-acting SSRI — delays ejaculation
- **Dosage:** 1-3 hours before activity (as prescribed)
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹200 per pack
- **Note:** Prescription-only; may cause nausea/dizziness

---

**Finasteride**
- **Salt/Composition:** Finasteride 1mg (hair) / 5mg (prostate)
- **Used For:** Male-pattern hair loss and prostate enlargement (BPH)
- **How It Works:** Blocks DHT hormone
- **Dosage:** 1mg (hair) or 5mg (prostate) once daily
- **Cost:** ₹80-₹250 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** Takes 3-6 months for results; not to be handled by pregnant women

---

**Tamsulosin**
- **Salt/Composition:** Tamsulosin 0.4mg
- **Used For:** Urination problems from enlarged prostate (BPH)
- **How It Works:** Alpha-blocker — relaxes prostate/bladder muscles
- **Dosage:** 0.4mg once daily after the same meal
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹150 per 10 tablets
- **Note:** May cause dizziness on standing (low BP)

---

## <Highlight color='#00A896' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 🩹 ANTISEPTICS & TOPICAL SKIN MEDICINES </Highlight>

**Betadine Ointment (Povidone-Iodine)**
- **Salt/Composition:** Povidone-Iodine 5% / 10%
- **Used For:** Wound antiseptic, cuts, minor burns, infection prevention
- **How It Works:** Broad antiseptic — kills bacteria, fungi, viruses
- **Dosage:** Apply to clean wound 1-2 times daily
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹150 per tube
- **Note:** For external use; avoid on large areas long-term

---

**Neomycin Cream**
- **Salt/Composition:** Neomycin (often with Bacitracin/Polymyxin)
- **Used For:** Minor skin infections, infected cuts
- **How It Works:** Topical antibiotic — kills skin bacteria
- **Dosage:** Apply thin layer 2-3 times daily
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹100 per tube
- **Note:** Stop if irritation/allergy develops

---

**Silver Sulfadiazine**
- **Salt/Composition:** Silver Sulfadiazine 1% cream
- **Used For:** Burns (prevents/treats burn-wound infection)
- **How It Works:** Antimicrobial — protects burnt skin from infection
- **Dosage:** Apply to burn under sterile dressing as advised
- **Cost:** ₹80-₹200 per tube
- **Note:** Standard burn-care cream

---

**Hydrocortisone Cream**
- **Salt/Composition:** Hydrocortisone 1%
- **Used For:** Itching, allergy, mild eczema, rashes
- **How It Works:** Mild steroid — reduces inflammation and itch
- **Dosage:** Apply thin layer 1-2 times daily (short-term)
- **Cost:** ₹40-₹100 per tube
- **Note:** Low-potency steroid; avoid prolonged use on face

---

**Clobetasol Cream**
- **Salt/Composition:** Clobetasol Propionate 0.05%
- **Used For:** Severe skin inflammation (psoriasis, stubborn eczema)
- **How It Works:** Very potent steroid — powerful anti-inflammatory
- **Dosage:** Thin layer once/twice daily, short-term only
- **Cost:** ₹60-₹150 per tube
- **Note:** Strong steroid — use only as prescribed; not for face/long-term

## <Highlight color='#8B0000' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> ⚠️ Important Safety Guidelines </Highlight>

**High-Alert Medicine Rules:**
- 🫀 **Heart & BP medicines:** Take daily, never stop suddenly
- 🩸 **Blood thinners:** Watch for unusual bleeding/bruising
- 🧠 **Mental-health medicines:** Taper slowly, only under doctor's advice
- 💊 **NSAIDs:** Always after food; caution with ulcers/kidney disease
- 🧴 **Strong steroid creams (Clobetasol):** Short-term, prescribed use only

**Always Check:**
- ✅ Expiry date and correct strength (mg)
- ✅ Drug interactions (tell your doctor ALL medicines/supplements)
- ✅ Correct dosage and timing
- ✅ Storage conditions

**See a Doctor Immediately If:**
- 🚨 Chest pain not relieved by rest/nitroglycerin
- 🚨 Signs of bleeding (black stools, blood in urine, easy bruising)
- 🚨 Severe allergic reaction (swelling, breathing difficulty)
- 🚨 Sudden mood changes or suicidal thoughts on psychiatric medicines

:::tip[💡 Pro Tips for Medicine Management]

1. **Know Your Salt Name:** Learn the generic (salt) name of your medicine, not just the brand — it helps when a brand is unavailable
2. **Regular Monitoring:** BP, sugar, INR (for warfarin), and cholesterol need periodic checks
3. **Medicine List:** Keep an updated list of all your medicines for every doctor visit
4. **Don't Double-Dose:** If you miss a dose, follow package advice — don't take two together
5. **Generic Saves Money:** Ask your pharmacist for generic equivalents (same salt, lower cost)
6. **Emergency Info:** Note down drug allergies and carry it with you

:::
