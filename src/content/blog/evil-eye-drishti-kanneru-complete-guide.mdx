# Evil Eye (Drishti) Complete Guide: கண்ணேறு, நாவூறு, காற்றூறு
# திருஷ்டி முழு வழிகாட்டி: கண்ணேறு, நாவூறு, காற்றூறு

The concept of **Evil Eye** or **Drishti (दृष्टि/திருஷ்டி)** is one of the most ancient and widespread beliefs across cultures worldwide. In Tamil culture, this manifests as **கண்ணேறு (Kanneru)**, along with related concepts like **நாவூறு (Naavuru)** and **காற்றூறு (Kaatru)**.

:::tip[Quick Overview - விரைவான கண்ணோட்டம்]
**Three Main Types of Afflictions:**

1. 👁️ **கண்ணேறு/கண்ணூறு (Kanneru)** - Evil Eye / Drishti Dosha
2. 👅 **நாவூறு (Naavuru)** - Tongue Affliction / Verbal Curse
3. 🌬️ **காற்றூறு (Kaatru)** - Wind/Air Affliction

**Universal Concept:** Found in Mediterranean, Middle Eastern, South Asian, Latin American, and African cultures with 60%+ global population believing in some form of evil eye.
:::

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> WHAT IS DRISHTI/EVIL EYE? - திருஷ்டி/கண்ணேறு என்றால் என்ன? </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The Concept Explained </Highlight>

**Drishti (दृष्टि/திருஷ்டி)** comes from Sanskrit root **"Drish" (दृश्)** meaning "to see" or "sight."

**Definition:**
Evil Eye is the belief that certain individuals can cause harm, illness, or bad luck to others through envious or admiring looks, even unintentionally.

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> How Does Evil Eye Work? </Highlight>

**Traditional Belief:**

According to traditional understanding, evil eye operates through:

**1. Energy Transfer:**
- Human eyes emit energy (subtle or electromagnetic)
- Strong emotions (envy, jealousy, excessive admiration) create negative energy
- This energy transfers through gaze to the recipient
- Affects aura/energy field of the person/object

**2. Intention vs. Unintentional:**

**Intentional Evil Eye (நோக்கமுடன்):**
- Deliberate ill-will or jealousy
- Conscious desire to harm
- Stronger and more harmful
- Takes longer to remove

**Unintentional Evil Eye (நோக்கமின்றி):**
- Excessive praise or admiration
- Strong focus without ill intent
- Common from loved ones ("Oh, your baby is SO beautiful!")
- Easier to remove

**3. Vulnerable Targets:**

Most susceptible to evil eye:
- 👶 **Babies and children** (pure, unprotected energy)
- 🤰 **Pregnant women** (delicate state)
- 💑 **Newlyweds** (happiness attracts envy)
- 🏆 **Successful people** (achievement breeds jealousy)
- 💰 **New acquisitions** (new car, house, jewelry)
- 🌱 **Plants and animals** (living beings with energy)

- Through jealous thoughts
- Without direct contact

**4. தொட்டு திருஷ்டி (Thottu Drishti) - Touch Drishti:**
- Through physical contact
- Touching with impure intentions

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> நாவூறு (NAAVURU) - TONGUE AFFLICTION </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is Naavuru? </Highlight>

**நாவூறு (Naavuru)** literally means "tongue penetration" or "tongue affliction."

**Definition:**
Harm caused through **spoken words** - curses, harsh criticism, gossip, or negative talk about someone.

**Tamil Saying:**
> "நாவினால் நரகம் புகும்" (Naavinaal Naragam Pugum)
> "Through the tongue, one enters hell"

**How It Differs from Evil Eye:**
- **Evil Eye:** Through gaze/sight
- **Naavuru:** Through speech/words
- **Mechanism:** Sound vibrations and verbal energy

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Types of Naavuru </Highlight>

**1. Direct Curse (சாபம் - Saabam):**
- Intentional curse words
- "May you suffer..."
- Especially powerful from:
  - Parents (பெற்றோர் சாபம்)
  - Spiritual people (ஞானி சாபம்)
  - Dying person's last words

**2. Harsh Criticism (கடுமையான விமர்சனம்):**
- Constant negative remarks
- "You'll never succeed"
- "You're worthless"
- Creates psychological programming

**3. Gossip (புறம் பேசுதல் - Puram Pesuthal):**
- Talking ill behind back
- Spreading rumors
- Character assassination
- Creates negative energy field

**4. Sarcasm with Ill Intent:**
- "Oh, you think you're so great?"
- Disguised jealousy
- Passive-aggressive remarks

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Symptoms of Naavuru </Highlight>

⚠️ **Signs:**
- Throat discomfort or pain
- Difficulty speaking
- Voice changes
- Communication problems
- Misunderstandings increasing
- Reputation damage
- Social isolation
- Projects failing due to others' interference
- Legal troubles from false accusations

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> காற்றூறு (KAATRU) - WIND/AIR AFFLICTION </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is Kaatru? </Highlight>

**காற்றூறு (Kaatru)** literally means "wind penetration" or "air affliction."

**Definition:**
Harm or illness attributed to:
- **Negative energy** carried by wind/air
- **Spirits or entities** moving through air
- **Environmental energies** at certain places/times
- **Atmospheric disturbances** affecting health

**Traditional Understanding:**

In Tamil culture, air/wind is believed to carry:
- Energies from places (cremation grounds, graveyards)
- Spirits (பேய், பிசாசு)
- Planetary influences (கிரக தோஷம்)
- Collective negative emotions

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Types of Kaatru </Highlight>

**1. இரவு காற்று (Iravu Kaatru) - Night Air:**
- Wind blowing at night
- Especially 12 AM - 3 AM (Brahma Muhurta reversed)
- Carries energies from other realms
- Traditional advice: Don't sleep under open sky at night

**2. சூனிய காற்று (Sooniya Kaatru) - Cemetery Wind:**
- Wind from burial/cremation grounds
- Carries death energy
- Passing such places: Hold breath, chant mantras

**3. கிரஹண காற்று (Grahana Kaatru) - Eclipse Wind:**
- Air during solar/lunar eclipse
- Considered highly inauspicious
- Don't go out, stay indoors
- Pregnant women especially careful

**4. விபத்து இடத்து காற்று (Vibaththu Idathu Kaatru) - Accident Site Wind:**
- Wind from accident/tragedy locations
- Sudden death creates energy disturbance
- Avoid if possible

---
