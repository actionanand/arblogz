## 🫘 Pulses / Lentils / Dal (பருப்பு / ಬೇಳೆ / दाल)

Pulses are dried seeds of legume plants. They are a staple protein source in Indian cuisine and come in various shapes, sizes, and colors.

### 🔍 How to Identify Different Pulses

**Visual Identification Guide:**

1. **Size**: Ranges from tiny (urad dal) to large (rajma kidney beans)
2. **Shape**: Round, oval, or kidney-shaped
3. **Color**: Yellow, orange, red, green, brown, black, or white
4. **Split vs Whole**: Some are sold whole (with skin), some split (husked)
5. **Texture**: Smooth or rough exterior

:::note[Split vs Whole]
- **Split (Dal)**: Husked and split in half - cooks faster, easier to digest
- **Whole (Saabut)**: With skin intact - more fiber, takes longer to cook
:::

## 📝 Shopping Tips

:::tip[Buying Pulses]
- **Freshness**: Look for bright, uniform color
- **Texture**: Should be dry, not moist
- **Smell**: Fresh, nutty aroma (no musty smell)
- **Check for**: Insects, stones, or debris
- **Storage**: Store in airtight containers in a cool, dry place
:::

:::note[Identifying Quality Spices]
- **Whole spices**: More aromatic than powdered
- **Color**: Vibrant, not faded
- **Aroma**: Strong and fresh
- **Texture**: Dry and crisp (for whole spices)
- **Check expiry**: Spices lose potency over time
:::
