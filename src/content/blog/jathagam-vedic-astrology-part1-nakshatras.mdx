## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is Jathagam? - ஜாதகம் என்றால் என்ன? </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Definition and Etymology </Highlight>

**Jathagam (ஜாதகம்)** comes from Sanskrit **Jātaka (जातक)**:
- **जात (Jāta)** = Born, birth
- **जातक (Jātaka)** = Related to birth, birth chart

**Tamil/Malayalam Names:**
- **ஜாதகம் (Jāthagam)** - Tamil
- **ജാതകം (Jātakam)** - Malayalam
- Also called **குண்டலி (Kuṇḍali)** or **ராசி பலன் (Rāsi Palan)**

**What is it?**

A Jathagam is a **snapshot of the sky** at the exact moment and place of your birth, showing:
- Position of 9 planets (Navagrahas)
- Position of Moon in one of 27 Nakshatras (stars)
- Ascendant (Lagna) - Rising sign
- 12 houses (Bhavas) representing different life areas
- Planetary periods (Dashas) throughout life

**Key Differences:**

- Same 27 Nakshatras

- Vimshottari Dasha system

This guide covers **both Tamil and Kerala traditions** and is applicable to all South Indian Jathagam readings.

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The 27 Nakshatras - 27 நட்சத்திரங்கள் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What are Nakshatras? </Highlight>

**Nakshatra (நட்சத்திரம்)** means "star" or "lunar mansion."

The zodiac (360°) is divided into **27 equal parts** of 13°20' each. Each Nakshatra represents:
- A specific segment of the sky
- A ruling deity
- A symbolic animal (Yoni)
- Specific characteristics and attributes

**Your Nakshatra** is determined by the **Moon's position** at the time of your birth.

**Importance in Jathagam:**
- Determines your fundamental nature and personality
- Used for name selection (Nakshatra Akshar)
- Marriage compatibility (Porutham/Kuta)
- Timing of events (Muhurtham)
- Dasha periods calculation

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Guna, Gana, and Nadi Classification </Highlight>

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Gayatri Mantras for 27 Nakshatras - நட்சத்திர காயத்ரி மந்திரங்கள் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Powerful Mantras for Each Nakshatra </Highlight>

Each Nakshatra has a specific **Gayatri Mantra** for its presiding deity. Chanting these mantras brings prosperity, removes afflictions, and invokes divine blessings.

**Note:** The words **Vidmahe (வித்மஹே)**, **Dhimahi (தீமஹி)**, **Tanno (தன்னோ)**, and **Prachodayat (ப்ரசோதயாத்)** are standard Gayatri framework words meaning: "We meditate upon," "May we contemplate," "May that [deity]," and "Inspire/guide us." Only deity-specific word meanings are provided below.

---

### **1. Ashwini (அஸ்வினி) - Saraswati Devi (சரஸ்வதி தேவி)**

**Tamil:**
```
ஓம் வாக்தேவ்யைச வித்மஹே
பிரஹ்மபத்ன்யைச தீமஹி
தன்னோ வாணீஹ் ப்ரசோதயாத்
```

**Devanagari:**
```
ॐ वाग्देव्यै च विद्महे
ब्रह्मपत्न्यै च धीमहि
तन्नो वाणी प्रचोदयात्
```

**Romanized (IAST):**
```
Om Vāgdevyai ca vidmahe
Brahmapatnyai ca dhīmahi
Tanno Vāṇī prachodayāt
```

**Word Meanings:**
- **Vāgdevyai (वाग्देव्यै)** = To the Goddess of Speech (Vāk = speech, Devī = goddess)
- **Brahmapatnyai (ब्रह्मपत्न्यै)** = To the consort of Brahma (Brahma = Creator God, Patnī = wife)
- **Vāṇī (वाणी)** = Goddess of speech and learning (another name for Saraswati)

---

### **2. Bharani (பரணி) - Durga Devi (துர்கா தேவி) - Eight-Armed**

**Tamil:**
```

கன்யாகுமாரி தீமஹி

```

**Devanagari:**
```

कन्याकुमारि धीमहि
तन्नो दुर्गा प्रचोदयात्
```

**Romanized (IAST):**
```
Om Kātyāyanāya vidmahe
Kanyākumāri dhīmahi
Tanno Durgā prachodayāt
```

**Word Meanings:**
- **Kātyāyanāya (कात्यायनाय)** = To Katyayani (form of Durga, daughter of sage Katyayana)
- **Kanyākumāri (कन्याकुमारि)** = The virgin maiden, eternal virgin goddess
- **Durgā (दुर्गा)** = The invincible one, destroyer of demons (Dur = difficult, Gā = to go beyond)

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Abhijit Nakshatra - அபிஜித் நட்சத்திரம் (28th Star) </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The Special 28th Nakshatra </Highlight>

**Abhijit (अभिजित्/அபிஜித்)** is a **special, intercalary Nakshatra** that exists between **Uttara Ashadha (உத்திராடம்) and Shravana (திருவோணம்)**.

**Why 28 Nakshatras?**

While the main system uses **27 Nakshatras**, ancient Vedic texts also mention **Abhijit** as the **28th Nakshatra**.

**Position:**
- Occupies the **last pada (4th) of Uttara Ashadha** and **first pada (1st) of Shravana**
- Spans approximately **6°40' to 10°53'20"** in Capricorn (Makara Rasi)
- Central star: **Vega** (Alpha Lyrae/வேகா) - one of the brightest stars in the sky

**Sanskrit Meaning:**
- **Abhijit (अभिजित्)** = "Victorious," "Conqueror," "The One who cannot be defeated"
- Root: **Abhi (अभि)** = towards, over + **Jit (जित)** = conquered, victorious

**Ruling Deity:** **Brahma (பிரம்மா)** - The Creator

**Characteristics:**
- **Most auspicious** Nakshatra
- **Self-luminous**, independent nature
- Brings **victory and success**
- Associated with **truthfulness and dharma**

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Why Abhijit is Not in Daily Use </Highlight>

**Historical Context:**

According to **Mahabharata**:
- During the **Kurukshetra war**, when Krishna wanted to ensure victory for the Pandavas, he **stopped time** when the sun was in Abhijit Nakshatra
- This guaranteed victory (Abhijit = victorious)
- Since then, Abhijit became so auspicious that it's considered **always favorable** - no need to calculate it separately

**Modern Usage:**

**For Muhurtham (Auspicious Timing):**
- **Abhijit Muhurtham** is calculated as the **middle 1/15th part of the day**
- Approximately **48 minutes around midday** (noon)
- This time is **always auspicious** regardless of Tithi, Vara, or Nakshatra
- Used for emergency auspicious work when proper Muhurtham is not available

**Calculation:**
```
Sunrise to Sunset = Total day duration
Divide by 15 parts = Each part is 1 Muhurtham
8th Muhurtham (middle) = Abhijit Muhurtham
```

**Example:**
- Sunrise: 6:00 AM
- Sunset: 6:00 PM
- Day duration: 12 hours = 720 minutes
- Each Muhurtham: 720 ÷ 15 = 48 minutes
- 8th Muhurtham: 11:36 AM to 12:24 PM = **Abhijit Muhurtham**

**When to Use Abhijit Muhurtham:**
- Starting new ventures
- Signing important documents
- Property registration
- Business inaugurations
- Emergency auspicious work
- When proper Muhurtham is not available

**Exception:**
- **Avoid Abhijit on Wednesdays** (considered inauspicious combination in some traditions)
- Some also avoid on **Saturdays**

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Why 27 Nakshatras, Not 28? </Highlight>

**Mathematical Reason:**
- The Moon's sidereal period = **27.32 days**
- 360° zodiac ÷ 27 Nakshatras = **13°20' per Nakshatra** (perfect division)
- 360° ÷ 28 = 12°51'26" (not a clean division)

**Practical Reason:**
- Abhijit is **embedded within** Uttara Ashadha and Shravana
- It's not a separate full Nakshatra but a **special segment**
- For daily Panchangam, using 27 makes calculations easier

**Spiritual Reason:**
- **27 = Sacred number** in Vedic tradition
- 27 = 3 × 9 (both sacred numbers)
- Represents the **27 Yoga Taras** (wives of Moon/Chandra)

**Current Practice:**
- Jathagams use **27 Nakshatras** for birth chart
- **Abhijit Muhurtham** used separately for timing auspicious events
- Best of both systems!

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Important Time Units Explained </Highlight>

#### **1. Nāḻikai (நாழிகை) - The Water Clock**

**Most Important Unit in Tamil Astrology!**

**Duration:** **24 minutes**

**Origin:**
- Ancient **water clock (Ghaṭikā)** that drips water
- Time taken for a specific quantity of water to drip through a small hole
- Still used in traditional Tamil Panchangams

**Usage:**
- Sunrise/sunset timings given in Nāḻikai
- Rāhu Kālam, Yama Gaṇṭam calculated in Nāḻikai
- Muhūrtam timing calculated in Nāḻikai

**Example:**
- "Sunrise at 15 Nāḻikai" = 15 × 24 minutes = 360 minutes = 6:00 AM (from midnight)

---

#### **2. Viṇāḻikai (விநாழிகை) - 1/60th of Nāḻikai**

**Duration:** **24 seconds** (or 0.4 minutes)

**Usage:**
- Very precise timing in Panchangam
- Planet transits calculated to Viṇāḻikai accuracy

**Calculation:**
- 1 Nāḻikai = 60 Viṇāḻikai
- 1 Viṇāḻikai = 24 seconds

---

#### **3. Muhūrttam (முகூர்த்தம்) - Auspicious Time**

**Duration:** **48 minutes** (2 Nāḻikai)

**Daily Division:**
- **15 Muhūrttams in daytime** (sunrise to sunset)
- **15 Muhūrttams in nighttime** (sunset to sunrise)
- **Total: 30 Muhūrttams in 24 hours**

**Auspicious Muhūrttams:**
1. **Abhijit Muhūrttam** - 8th Muhūrttam of the day (around noon) - Most auspicious
2. **Vijaya Muhūrttam** - Victory time
3. **Brahma Muhūrttam** - 2 Muhūrttams before sunrise (~4:30-5:30 AM) - Best for meditation

**Calculation:**
```
Day duration (sunrise to sunset) ÷ 15 = Each Muhūrttam duration
Example: 12 hours ÷ 15 = 48 minutes per Muhūrttam
```

---

#### **4. Sāmam/Yāmam (சாமம்/ஜாமம்) - Time Watch**

**Duration:** **3 hours** (7.5 Nāḻikai)

**8 Yāmas in a Day:**

**Daytime (4 Yāmas):**
1. **Pratah Yāma (காலை ஜாமம்)** - Morning watch (~6 AM - 9 AM)
2. **Saṅgava Yāma (முற்பகல் ஜாமம்)** - Forenoon watch (~9 AM - 12 PM)
3. **Madhyāhna Yāma (மத்தியான ஜாமம்)** - Midday watch (~12 PM - 3 PM)
4. **Aparāhṇa Yāma (பிற்பகல் ஜாமம்)** - Afternoon watch (~3 PM - 6 PM)

**Nighttime (4 Yāmas):**
5. **Sayāhna Yāma (மாலை ஜாமம்)** - Evening watch (~6 PM - 9 PM)
6. **Pradoṣa Yāma (முதல் இரவு ஜாமம்)** - Early night watch (~9 PM - 12 AM)
7. **Niśītha Yāma (நள்ளிரவு ஜாமம்)** - Midnight watch (~12 AM - 3 AM)
8. **Uṣā Yāma (அதி காலை ஜாமம்)** - Pre-dawn watch (~3 AM - 6 AM)

**Usage:**
- Traditional time-keeping
- Temple rituals scheduled by Yāmas
- Rāhu Kālam falls in specific Yāmas on each day

---

#### **5. Pakṣa (பக்ஷம்) - Lunar Fortnight**

**Duration:** **15 Tithis (lunar days)** ≈ 14-15 solar days

**Two types:**
1. **Śukla Pakṣa (சுக்ல பக்ஷம்)** = Bright fortnight (waxing moon) - Pratipad to Pūrṇimā (Full Moon)
2. **Kṛṣṇa Pakṣa (கிருஷ்ண பக்ஷம்)** = Dark fortnight (waning moon) - Pratipad to Amāvāsyā (New Moon)

**Usage:**
- Auspicious activities in Śukla Pakṣa (growing moon)
- Destructive/removing activities in Kṛṣṇa Pakṣa (diminishing moon)

---

#### **6. Ṛtu (ருது/பருவம்) - Six Seasons**

**Duration:** **2 months each**

**Six Seasons (ஆறு பருவங்கள்):**

| **2. Grīṣma** | கிரீஷ்ம காலம் (கோடை) | Jyeṣṭha-Āṣāḍha<br/>(ஆனி-ஆடி)<br/>Mid-May to Mid-July | Summer - Hot season |
| **3. Varṣā** | வர்ஷா காலம் (மழை) | Śrāvaṇa-Bhādrapada<br/>(ஆவணி-புரட்டாசி)<br/>Mid-July to Mid-Sept | Monsoon - Rainy season |
| **4. Śarad** | சரத் காலம் (இலையுதிர்) | Āśvina-Kārtika<br/>(ஐப்பசி-கார்த்திகை)<br/>Mid-Sept to Mid-Nov | Autumn - Post-monsoon |

---

#### **7. Ayana (ஆயனம்) - Solar Movement**

**Duration:** **6 months** (3 Ṛtus)

**Two Ayanas:**

**1. Uttarāyaṇa (உத்தராயணம்)**
- **Northward movement of Sun**
- **Duration:** Makara Saṅkrānti (mid-January) to Karka Saṅkrānti (mid-July)
- **Seasons:** Śiśira, Vasanta, Grīṣma
- **Characteristics:** **Auspicious, positive energy, day-time of gods**
- **Activities:** Marriages, new beginnings, spiritual practices highly beneficial

**2. Dakṣiṇāyana (தக்ஷிணாயனம்)**
- **Southward movement of Sun**
- **Duration:** Karka Saṅkrānti (mid-July) to Makara Saṅkrānti (mid-January)
- **Seasons:** Varṣā, Śarad, Hemanta
- **Characteristics:** **Night-time of gods, introspection period**
- **Activities:** Ancestor worship, pitṛ kārya, spiritual sadhana

**Astrological Importance:**
- Uttarāyaṇa considered more auspicious for most activities
- Pitṛ śrāddha (ancestor rituals) done in Dakṣiṇāyana
- Death in Uttarāyaṇa believed to grant easier liberation
