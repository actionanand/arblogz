## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is Jathagam? - ஜாதகம் என்றால் என்ன? </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Definition and Etymology </Highlight>

**Jathagam (ஜாதகம்)** comes from Sanskrit **Jātaka (जातक)**:
- **जात (Jāta)** = Born, birth
- **जातक (Jātaka)** = Related to birth, birth chart

**Tamil/Malayalam Names:**
- **ஜாதகம் (Jāthagam)** - Tamil
- **ജാതകം (Jātakam)** - Malayalam
- Also called **குண்டலி (Kuṇḍali)** or **ராசி பலன் (Rāsi Palan)**

**What is it?**

A Jathagam is a **snapshot of the sky** at the exact moment and place of your birth, showing:
- Position of 9 planets (Navagrahas)
- Position of Moon in one of 27 Nakshatras (stars)
- Ascendant (Lagna) - Rising sign
- 12 houses (Bhavas) representing different life areas
- Planetary periods (Dashas) throughout life

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Tamil vs Kerala Jathagam Format </Highlight>

**Key Differences:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Aspect | Tamil Method | Kerala Method |

**Both methods use:**
- Same 27 Nakshatras
- Same 12 Rashis (Zodiac signs)
- Same 9 Planets (Navagrahas)
- Vimshottari Dasha system

This guide covers **both Tamil and Kerala traditions** and is applicable to all South Indian Jathagam readings.

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The 27 Nakshatras - 27 நட்சத்திரங்கள் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What are Nakshatras? </Highlight>

**Nakshatra (நட்சத்திரம்)** means "star" or "lunar mansion."

The zodiac (360°) is divided into **27 equal parts** of 13°20' each. Each Nakshatra represents:
- A specific segment of the sky
- A ruling deity
- A symbolic animal (Yoni)
- Specific characteristics and attributes

**Your Nakshatra** is determined by the **Moon's position** at the time of your birth.

**Importance in Jathagam:**
- Determines your fundamental nature and personality
- Used for name selection (Nakshatra Akshar)
- Marriage compatibility (Porutham/Kuta)
- Timing of events (Muhurtham)
- Dasha periods calculation

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Guna, Gana, and Nadi Classification </Highlight>

Each Nakshatra is classified by:

**1. Guna (Quality) - குணம்**
