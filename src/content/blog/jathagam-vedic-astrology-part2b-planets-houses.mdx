## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Planetary States - கிரக நிலைகள் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Understanding Planetary Strength </Highlight>

A planet's strength in a Jathagam depends on its **position** in a Rasi. There are several states:

**1. Uccha (Exaltation) - உச்ச நிலை**
- Planet is in its **strongest** position
- Gives maximum beneficial results
- Like a king on his throne

**2. Neecha (Debilitation) - நீச நிலை**
- Planet is in its **weakest** position
- Struggles to give results
- Like a king in prison

**3. Moolatrikona - மூலத்திரிகோணம்**
- Planet's **own throne** within its sign
- Almost as strong as exaltation
- Comfortable and powerful

**4. Swa-Rasi (Own Sign) - சொந்த ராசி**
- Planet in its **own house**
- Strong and comfortable
- Like being at home

**5. Friend's Sign - நண்பர் ராசி**
- Planet in a friendly sign
- Moderately strong
- Like visiting a friend's house

**6. Neutral Sign - நடுநிலை ராசி**
- Neither good nor bad
- Moderate results

**7. Enemy's Sign - எதிரி ராசி**
- Uncomfortable position
- Weak results
- Like being in hostile territory

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Why Planetary Friendships Matter </Highlight>

   - Enemy planets aspecting = Creates tension

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The 12 Bhavas (Houses) - 12 வீடுகள் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What are Bhavas? </Highlight>

**Bhava (பாவம்)** means "house" or "abode" in Sanskrit.

The Jathagam chart is divided into **12 houses**, each representing different areas of life. The houses are **fixed** based on the Lagna (Ascendant), which is the 1st house.

**Important Concepts:**

- **Lagna (லக்னம்)** = 1st House = Ascendant = Rising sign at time of birth
- Houses rotate **counter-clockwise** in South Indian chart
- Each house represents specific life areas
- Planets placed in houses influence those areas

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> House Classifications </Highlight>

**By Nature - இயல்பு வகை:**

**1. Kendras (கேந்திரங்கள்) - Angular Houses (Pillars)**
- Houses: 1, 4, 7, 10
- Most powerful houses (விஷ்ணு ஸ்தானம் - Vishnu Sthana)
- Planets here give strong results
- Like four pillars supporting life

**2. Trikonas (திரிகோணங்கள்) - Trinal Houses (Trine)**
- Houses: 1, 5, 9
- Houses of Dharma (தர்ம வீடுகள்)
- Most auspicious houses (லக்ஷ்மி ஸ்தானம் - Lakshmi Sthana)
- Bring luck and fortune

**3. Upachayas (உபசயங்கள்) - Growing Houses**
- Houses: 3, 6, 10, 11
- Improve with time and effort
- Malefic planets do well here
- Growth-oriented areas

**4. Dusthanas (துஷ்டானங்கள்) - Difficult Houses**
- Houses: 6, 8, 12
- Challenging areas of life
- Malefic planets worsen these
- Need remedies and hard work

**5. Marakas (மாரகங்கள்) - Death-Inflicting Houses**
- Houses: 2, 7
- Can cause death during their Dasha
- Need careful analysis

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Benefic vs Malefic Planets </Highlight>

**Natural Benefics (சுப கிரகங்கள்):**
- **Jupiter (குரு)** - Great benefic
- **Venus (சுக்கிரன்)** - Lesser benefic
- **Mercury (புதன்)** - Benefic when alone or with benefics
- **Waxing Moon (பூரண சந்திரன்)** - Bright Moon

**Natural Malefics (பாப கிரகங்கள்):**
- **Saturn (சனி)** - Great malefic
- **Mars (செவ்வாய்)** - Lesser malefic
- **Rahu & Ketu (ராகு & கேது)** - Shadow malefics
- **Sun (சூரியன்)** - Mild malefic (separative)
- **Waning Moon (அமாவாசை சந்திரன்)** - Dark Moon

**Important Notes:**
- Malefics in **Upachaya houses (3, 6, 10, 11)** = Good (improve with time)
- Malefics in **Trikona houses (1, 5, 9)** = Problematic
- Benefics in **Dusthanas (6, 8, 12)** = Waste their goodness
- Context matters - lordship changes benefic/malefic nature

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Introduction to Major Doshas - தோஷங்களின் அறிமுகம் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is a Dosha? </Highlight>

**Dosha (தோஷம்)** means "defect" or "affliction" in Sanskrit.

A Dosha occurs when planets are placed in certain **unfavorable positions** that create challenges in specific life areas. **Not all Doshas are bad** - some have exceptions and cancellations.

**Common Doshas we'll cover in Part 4:**

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 1. Chevvai Dosham (Manglik Dosha) - செவ்வாய் தோஷம் </Highlight>

**Quick Overview:**

**When it occurs:**
- Mars in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12 from Lagna
- Also checked from Moon and Venus

**Effects:**
- Delays or problems in marriage
- Conflicts with spouse
- Possible separation or widowhood (in severe cases)

**Severity:**
- Partial Chevvai: Mars in 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12 (less severe)
- Full Chevvai: Mars in specific degrees and combinations

**Cancellations:**
- If both bride and groom have Chevvai Dosham
- If Mars is in own/exalted sign
- Certain age exceptions (after 28 years)
- **Detailed cancellations and remedies in Part 4**

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 2. Sade Sati (7½ Sani) - ஏழரை சனி </Highlight>

**Quick Overview:**

**When it occurs:**
- Saturn transiting through:
  - 12th house from your Moon Rasi
  - Your Moon Rasi itself
  - 2nd house from your Moon Rasi
- Total duration: 7.5 years

**Three Phases:**
1. **Arohanam (ஏறும் சனி)** - Saturn in 12th = Rising phase (Most difficult)
2. **Madhya (நடுவில் சனி)** - Saturn in your Rasi = Peak phase
3. **Avarohanam (இறங்கும் சனி)** - Saturn in 2nd = Setting phase (Easing)

**Effects:**
- Delays, obstacles, struggles
- Health issues, mental stress
- Financial difficulties
- Career challenges
- BUT also brings maturity and wisdom

**Who's Currently in Sade Sati (as of 2026):**
- Saturn is in Aquarius (கும்பம்)
- Sade Sati affects: Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces Rasis
- **Full timing and remedies in Part 4**

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 3. Kaal Sarpa Dosha - காலசர்ப தோஷம் </Highlight>

**Quick Overview:**

**When it occurs:**
- All 7 planets (Sun to Saturn) are between Rahu and Ketu
- Forms a "serpent" formation in the chart

**12 Types:**
Based on which houses Rahu and Ketu occupy (12 different types - each with different effects)

**Effects:**
- Delays in success despite efforts
- Mental anxiety, bad dreams
- Health issues
- Relationship challenges
- BUT also gives sudden success after struggle

**Partial vs Full:**
- **Full Kaal Sarpa:** All planets strictly between Rahu-Ketu
- **Partial:** One or more planets outside the axis (less severe)

**Important:** Many people have this, and it's not always bad!
- **Detailed 12 types and remedies in Part 4**
