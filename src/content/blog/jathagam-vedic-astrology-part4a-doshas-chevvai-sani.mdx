## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Understanding Doshas - தோஷங்களை புரிந்துகொள்ளல் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is a Dosha? </Highlight>

**Dosha (दोष/தோஷம்)** literally means **"fault," "defect," or "affliction"** in Sanskrit.

**Simple Explanation:**
A dosha is an **imbalance or affliction** in the birth chart that can create challenges in specific areas of life.

**Important Truth:**
- ❌ Doshas are **NOT curses** or punishments
- ❌ Doshas are **NOT permanent problems**
- ✅ Doshas are **karmic indicators** from past lives
- ✅ Doshas **CAN be remedied** through proper actions
- ✅ Many doshas have **natural cancellations**

**Common Misconception:**
"If I have Chevvai Dosham, I'll never marry happily" ← **FALSE!**

**Reality:**
Chevvai Dosham can be cancelled, balanced, or remedied. Millions of people with Chevvai Dosham have successful marriages!

**4. Ancestral Doshas:**

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> CHEVVAI DOSHAM - செவ்வாய் தோஷம் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is Chevvai Dosham? </Highlight>

**Chevvai Dosham (செவ்வாய் தோஷம்)** is also called:
- **Mangal Dosha** (मङ्गल दोष) in North India
- **Kuja Dosha** (कुज दोष) in Sanskrit
- **Angaraka Dosha** in some regions

**Definition:**
When **Mars (Chevvai/செவ்வாய்)** is placed in certain houses from **Lagna (Ascendant)**, it creates Chevvai Dosham.

**Why it's feared:**
Chevvai Dosham primarily affects **married life**, causing:
- Conflicts with spouse
- Delays in marriage
- Separation or divorce
- Spouse's health issues
- Early widowhood (in severe cases)

**Important Context:**
Mars is a **fiery, aggressive planet** (Bhatru Karaka - sibling significator, warrior energy). When placed in marriage-related houses, its aggressive nature can harm marital harmony.

---

Chevvai Dosham is formed when Mars is in **6 specific houses** from Lagna:

**Most Dangerous Positions:**
1. **8th House** - Affects spouse's longevity (most feared)
2. **7th House** - Direct affliction to marriage house
3. **1st House** - Self's aggressive nature

**Important Note:**
Some astrologers also check Mars from **Moon (Chandra Lagna)** and **Venus (Shukra)**. A person can have:
- Chevvai Dosham from Lagna only
- Chevvai Dosham from Moon only
- Chevvai Dosham from both (stronger dosha)
- No dosha from either (blessed!)

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Full vs Partial Chevvai Dosham </Highlight>

**Not all Chevvai Doshas are equal!** There are levels:

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Type | Description | Marriage Impact | Remedy Need |
|------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Full/Double Dosham<br/>முழு தோஷம்** | Mars in dosha houses from **BOTH Lagna & Moon** | Severe - major conflicts, separation risk | Essential |
| **Single Dosham<br/>ஒற்றை தோஷம்** | Mars in dosha houses from **Lagna OR Moon** only | Moderate - manageable conflicts | Recommended |
| **Anshik (Partial)<br/>பகுதி தோஷம்** | Mars in 1st/2nd/4th/12th (less severe houses) | Mild - minor disagreements | Optional |
| **No Dosham<br/>தோஷம் இல்லை** | Mars in 3rd, 5th, 6th, 9th, 10th, 11th houses | No affliction | None needed |

</div>

**Example 1: Full Dosham**
- Mars in 7th from Lagna (Libra Lagna, Mars in Aries 7th)
- Mars in 8th from Moon (Moon in Virgo, Mars in Aries 8th)
- **Result:** Double affliction - needs strong remedies

**Example 2: Partial Dosham**
- Mars in 2nd from Lagna (harsh speech tendency)
- Mars in 10th from Moon (no dosha)
- **Result:** Mild dosha - speech awareness needed

**Great News!** Chevvai Dosham has **many natural cancellations**. These are called **Dosha Bhangam (தோஷ பங்கம்)**.

**If ANY of these conditions apply, Chevvai Dosham is CANCELLED or GREATLY REDUCED:**

#### **1. Age-Based Cancellation (வயது முறை)**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

**Why?** Mars matures at age 28. After this, its aggressive energy becomes disciplined.

**Practical Advice:** If you have Chevvai Dosham, marrying after 28 is beneficial!

**If BOTH bride and groom have Chevvai Dosham:**
- ✅ Dosha is **100% CANCELLED**

- ✅ No remedies needed

The dosha must be from **same reference point**:
- Both from Lagna ✅
- Both from Moon ✅
- Both from Venus ✅
- One from Lagna, one from Moon ❌ (doesn't cancel fully)

**Example:**
- Groom: Mars in 7th from Lagna
- Bride: Mars in 8th from Lagna
- **Result:** Both have dosha from Lagna → CANCELLED!

**Worry if:**

- ✅ Mars is **debilitated** (in Cancer) in 7th/8th

- ❌ Both partners have dosha
- ❌ Mars in own sign/exalted
- ❌ Strong benefic aspects present
- ❌ Mars only from Moon, not from Lagna (less severe)

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> 7½ SANI - ஏழரை சனி </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What is 7½ Sani? </Highlight>

**7½ Sani (ஏழரை சனி)** is also called:
- **Ezharai Shani** (Tamil - எழரை சனி)
- **Sade Sati** (साढ़े साती) in Hindi
- **Elarai Sani** in Malayalam
- **7½ Years Saturn Transit**

**Definition:**
When **Saturn (Shani/சனி)** transits through the **12th, 1st, and 2nd houses from your Moon sign (Rasi/ராசி)**, this period is called 7½ Sani.

**Duration:** Approximately **7½ years** (2.5 years in each of the 3 houses)

**Why it's feared:**
Saturn is the **slowest planet** and represents:
- Karma (கர்மா)
- Discipline and hard work
- Delays and obstacles
- Suffering and lessons
- Justice and truth

During 7½ Sani, life becomes **challenging** - you face your karmic debts!

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The Three Phases - மூன்று பருவங்கள் </Highlight>

7½ Sani is divided into **3 phases**, each with different effects:

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| **2nd Phase<br/>Madhya<br/>மத்யம** | **1st house** on Moon | **Peak Phase** | 2.5 years | **MOST DIFFICULT** - Health, identity crisis, obstacles, delays |
| **3rd Phase<br/>Avarohanam<br/>அவரோஹணம்** | **2nd house** from Moon | **Descending Phase** | 2.5 years | **Family issues**, wealth challenges, speech problems, recovery begins |

</div>

**Pattern:**
Each Rasi experiences 7½ Sani approximately **once every 30 years** (since Saturn takes ~30 years to complete zodiac).
