## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Understanding Divisional Charts - வர்க்க அட்டவணைகள் </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> What Are Divisional Charts? </Highlight>

**Varga (வர்க்கம்)** = Division or Part

**Concept:**
- Each Rasi (30°) is divided into smaller sections
- Each division creates a new chart
- Each chart reveals a specific life area in detail

**Why Divisional Charts?**

**Rasi Chart (D1) Limitation:**
- Shows broad overview of life
- Can't reveal fine details
- Like viewing from 10,000 feet altitude

**Divisional Charts:**
- Zoom into specific areas
- Reveal hidden strengths/weaknesses
- Like microscopic examination

**Example Analogy:**
- **D1 (Rasi):** Your complete resume
- **D9 (Navamsa):** Your character reference letter
- **D10 (Dasamsa):** Your career portfolio
- **D7 (Saptamsa):** Your family planning document
- **D60 (Shashtiamsa):** Your complete life story including past lives!

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> How Divisional Charts Work </Highlight>

**Calculation Example: D7 (Saptamsa)**

**Step 1:** Take planet's position in Rasi
- Example: **Sun at Aries 18°45'**

**Step 2:** Divide Rasi (30°) by division number (7)
- 30° ÷ 7 = 4°17' per division

**Step 3:** Find which division the planet falls in
- 18°45' ÷ 4°17' = 4.38 → **5th division**

**Step 4:** Apply starting sign rule
- Aries (odd sign) starts from **Aries itself** in D7
- 5th sign from Aries = **Leo**
- **Sun in D7 = Leo**

**Different Starting Points:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Rasi Type | D7 Starting Sign |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Odd signs (1,3,5,7,9,11) | Same sign |
| Even signs (2,4,6,8,10,12) | 7th sign from itself |

</div>

**Don't worry about manual calculation!** Software does this instantly. Just understand the concept.
