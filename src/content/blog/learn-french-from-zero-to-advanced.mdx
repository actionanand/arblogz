### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The French Alphabet & Pronunciation </Highlight>

French uses the same 26-letter alphabet as English, but pronunciation is very different!

| Z | zède | zed | **Z**éro (zero) |

</div>

:::caution[Pronunciation Traps for English Speakers]
**Critical Differences:**
- **R** is guttural (throat sound), NOT rolled like Spanish
- **U** is NOT "you" - pucker lips and say "ee"
- **H** is always silent in French words
- **J** sounds like "zh" in "pleasure"
- **Final consonants** are usually silent: Paris (pah-REE), temps (TAHN)
- **Nasal vowels**: an, en, in, on, un (air comes through nose!)
:::

:::note[Tu vs Vous - The Critical Distinction]
**TU** (informal):
- Family members
- Close friends
- Children
- Pets
- People your age in casual settings

**VOUS** (formal):
- Strangers
- Older people
- Professional settings
- Groups of people
- Shows respect

**Golden Rule**: When in doubt, use VOUS!

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Level 2: Elementary (A2) </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Gender & Articles - The Foundation </Highlight>

**Every French noun has a gender**: masculine (M) or feminine (F). There's NO neutral!

**Definite Articles** (the):

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| English | Masculine | Feminine | Plural | Before vowel |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|--------------|
| the | le (luh) | la (lah) | les (lay) | l' (l) |
| **Example** | le livre (book) | la table (table) | les livres (books) | l'ami (friend) |

</div>

**Indefinite Articles** (a, an, some):

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| English | Masculine | Feminine | Plural |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| a, an | un (uhn) | une (ewn) | des (day) |
| **Example** | un chat (a cat) | une maison (a house) | des chats (some cats) |

</div>

:::caution[Gender Rules - But Lots of Exceptions!]
**Generally Masculine:**
- Days, months, seasons, languages
- Trees, metals
- -age, -ment, -eau endings
- Example: le fromage (cheese), le moment, le bateau (boat)

**Generally Feminine:**
- Countries ending in -e
- -tion, -sion, -té, -ette endings
- Example: la nation, la beauté (beauty), la cigarette

**Must Memorize**: le/la with each noun! No shortcuts!
:::

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Essential Verbs - Present Tense </Highlight>

#### ÊTRE (to be) - Irregular & Most Important!

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| je | suis | swee | Je suis étudiant (I am a student) |
| tu | es | ay | Tu es français (You are French) |
| il/elle | est | ay | Elle est belle (She is beautiful) |
| nous | sommes | somm | Nous sommes amis (We are friends) |
| vous | êtes | et | Vous êtes gentil (You are kind) |
| ils/elles | sont | sohn | Ils sont contents (They are happy) |

</div>

#### AVOIR (to have) - Second Most Important!

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| je / j' | ai | ay | J'ai 25 ans (I am 25 years old) |
| tu | as | ah | Tu as raison (You are right) |
| il/elle | a | ah | Il a faim (He is hungry) |
| nous | avons | ah-VOHN | Nous avons une maison (We have a house) |
| vous | avez | ah-VAY | Vous avez tort (You are wrong) |
| ils/elles | ont | ohn | Elles ont peur (They are afraid) |

</div>

:::note[ÊTRE vs AVOIR - Critical for Expressions!]
French uses AVOIR for many states where English uses "to be":

**With AVOIR:**
- avoir faim/soif (to be hungry/thirsty)
- avoir chaud/froid (to be hot/cold)
- avoir peur (to be afraid)
- avoir raison/tort (to be right/wrong)
- avoir besoin de (to need)
- avoir envie de (to want/feel like)
- **avoir X ans** (to be X years old) - NOT "être"!

**With ÊTRE:**
- être content/triste (to be happy/sad)
- être malade (to be sick)
- être d'accord (to agree)
:::

#### Regular -ER Verbs (90% of French verbs!)

**PARLER (to speak)** - Model verb:

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Pronoun | Conjugation | Pronunciation | English |
|---------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| je | parle | parl | I speak |
| tu | parles | parl | you speak |
| il/elle | parle | parl | he/she speaks |
| nous | parlons | par-LOHN | we speak |
| vous | parlez | par-LAY | you speak |
| ils/elles | parlent | parl | they speak |

</div>

**Pattern**: Remove -ER, add: -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent

**Common -ER Verbs:**
- aimer (to like/love)
- donner (to give)
- manger (to eat)
- regarder (to watch)
- écouter (to listen)
- travailler (to work)
- habiter (to live)
- chercher (to look for)
- trouver (to find)
- penser (to think)

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Building Your First Sentences </Highlight>

**Basic Sentence Structure**: Subject + Verb + Object

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| French | Word-by-word | English |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| Je parle français | I speak French | I speak French |
| Tu aimes le café | You like the coffee | You like coffee |
| Elle regarde la télévision | She watches the television | She watches TV |
| Nous habitons à Paris | We live in Paris | We live in Paris |
| Vous mangez une pomme | You eat an apple | You eat an apple |
| Ils sont contents | They are happy | They are happy |

</div>

**Making Questions - 3 Ways:**

1. **Intonation** (easiest - just raise voice at end):
   - Tu parles français? (You speak French?)

2. **Est-ce que** (add to beginning):
   - Est-ce que tu parles français? (Do you speak French?)

3. **Inversion** (formal - swap subject and verb):
   - Parles-tu français? (Do you speak French?)

**Making Negatives** - ne...pas sandwich:

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Positive | Negative | English |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Je parle | Je ne parle pas | I don't speak |
| Tu aimes | Tu n'aimes pas | You don't like |
| Il est | Il n'est pas | He is not |
| Nous avons | Nous n'avons pas | We don't have |

</div>

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Level 3: Intermediate (B1-B2) </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Past Tenses - Passé Composé </Highlight>

**Most common past tense** - like English "have done"

**Formula**: AVOIR/ÊTRE (present) + Past Participle

**With AVOIR (most verbs):**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Infinitive | Past Participle | Example |
|------------|-----------------|---------|
| parler (speak) | parlé | J'ai parlé (I spoke/have spoken) |
| manger (eat) | mangé | Tu as mangé (You ate) |
| finir (finish) | fini | Il a fini (He finished) |
| vendre (sell) | vendu | Elle a vendu (She sold) |
| prendre (take) | pris | Nous avons pris (We took) |
| faire (do/make) | fait | Vous avez fait (You did) |
| voir (see) | vu | Ils ont vu (They saw) |
| avoir (have) | eu | J'ai eu (I had) |
| être (be) | été | Tu as été (You were) |

</div>

**With ÊTRE (DR & MRS VANDERTRAMP verbs):**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Infinitive | Past Participle | Example |
|------------|-----------------|---------|
| **D**evenir (become) | devenu | Je suis devenu(e) |
| **R**evenir (come back) | revenu | Tu es revenu(e) |
| **M**onter (go up) | monté | Il/Elle est monté(e) |
| **R**ester (stay) | resté | Nous sommes resté(e)s |
| **S**ortir (go out) | sorti | Vous êtes sorti(e)(s) |
| **V**enir (come) | venu | Ils/Elles sont venu(e)s |
| **A**ller (go) | allé | Je suis allé(e) |
| **N**aître (be born) | né | Tu es né(e) |
| **D**escendre (go down) | descendu | Il/Elle est descendu(e) |
| **E**ntrer (enter) | entré | Nous sommes entré(e)s |
| **R**entrer (return) | rentré | Vous êtes rentré(e)(s) |
| **T**omber (fall) | tombé | Ils/Elles sont tombé(e)s |
| **R**etourner (return) | retourné | Je suis retourné(e) |
| **A**rriver (arrive) | arrivé | Tu es arrivé(e) |
| **M**ourir (die) | mort | Il/Elle est mort(e) |
| **P**artir (leave) | parti | Nous sommes parti(e)s |

</div>

:::caution[Agreement with ÊTRE!]
When using ÊTRE, the past participle agrees with the subject:
- **Je suis allé** (I went - male speaker)
- **Je suis allée** (I went - female speaker)
- **Nous sommes allés** (We went - males or mixed group)
- **Nous sommes allées** (We went - all females)
- **Elle est partie** (She left - add 'e')
- **Ils sont partis** (They left - add 's')
:::

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Imparfait - Imperfect Past </Highlight>

**Used for**: habits, descriptions, ongoing actions in the past

**Formula**: nous form (present) - ons + imparfait endings

**Endings**: -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Pronoun | ÊTRE | AVOIR | PARLER |
|---------|------|-------|--------|
| je | étais | avais | parlais |
| tu | étais | avais | parlais |
| il/elle | était | avait | parlait |
| nous | étions | avions | parlions |
| vous | étiez | aviez | parliez |
| ils/elles | étaient | avaient | parlaient |

</div>

**Passé Composé vs Imparfait:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Passé Composé (Completed) | Imparfait (Ongoing/Habitual) |
|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| J'ai mangé (I ate - specific) | Je mangeais (I was eating / used to eat) |
| Il est parti (He left) | Il partait toujours à 8h (He always used to leave at 8) |
| Nous avons vu (We saw) | Il faisait beau (It was nice weather) |

</div>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Future Tenses </Highlight>

**1. Near Future (Futur Proche)** - going to...

**Formula**: ALLER (present) + infinitive

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Tu vas partir | You're going to leave |
| Il va étudier | He's going to study |
| Nous allons voyager | We're going to travel |
| Vous allez comprendre | You're going to understand |
| Elles vont arriver | They're going to arrive |

</div>

**2. Simple Future (Futur Simple)** - will...

**Formula**: Infinitive + future endings (-ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont)

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

|------------|----|----|---------|------|------|-----------|
| parler | parlerai | parleras | parlera | parlerons | parlerez | parleront |
| finir | finirai | finiras | finira | finirons | finirez | finiront |
| être | serai | seras | sera | serons | serez | seront |
| avoir | aurai | auras | aura | aurons | aurez | auront |
| aller | irai | iras | ira | irons | irez | iront |
| faire | ferai | feras | fera | ferons | ferez | feront |

</div>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Conditional Mood </Highlight>

**Used for**: polite requests, hypothetical situations, "would"

**Formula**: Future stem + imparfait endings

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| French | English | Usage |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Je voudrais | I would like | Polite request |
| Tu pourrais m'aider? | Could you help me? | Polite question |
| Il devrait étudier | He should study | Advice |
| Nous aimerions partir | We would like to leave | Desire |
| Vous pourriez répéter? | Could you repeat? | Polite request |

</div>

:::tip[Creative Learning Tip #5: Time Travel Storytelling]
**Master Tenses Through Narrative:**
1. **Present**: Describe your day - "Je me réveille, je mange..."
2. **Passé Composé**: Yesterday's adventure - "Hier, je suis allé au cinéma..."
3. **Imparfait**: Childhood memories - "Quand j'étais petit, je jouais..."
4. **Future**: Tomorrow's plans - "Demain, je vais visiter..."
5. Combine all: "Quand j'étais jeune (imparfait), j'ai rencontré (PC) mon ami. Maintenant je travaille (present) et demain je partirai (future)."
:::

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Level 4: Advanced (C1-C2) </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Subjunctive Mood - The Challenge </Highlight>

**Used for**: doubt, emotion, necessity, desire, uncertainty

**Trigger Phrases**: il faut que, je veux que, je doute que, bien que...

**Formation**: ils/elles form (present) - ent + subjunctive endings

**Endings**: -e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Regular Example (parler) | Irregular (être) | Irregular (avoir) |
|--------------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| que je parle | que je sois | que j'aie |
| que tu parles | que tu sois | que tu aies |
| qu'il/elle parle | qu'il/elle soit | qu'il/elle ait |
| que nous parlions | que nous soyons | que nous ayons |
| que vous parliez | que vous soyez | que vous ayez |
| qu'ils/elles parlent | qu'ils/elles soient | qu'ils/elles aient |

</div>

**Common Subjunctive Uses:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| French | English | Why Subjunctive |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Il faut que tu viennes | You must come | Necessity |
| Je veux qu'il parte | I want him to leave | Desire |
| Je doute qu'elle soit là | I doubt she's there | Doubt |
| Bien qu'il pleuve | Although it's raining | Concession |
| Avant qu'il arrive | Before he arrives | Time + uncertainty |

</div>

:::caution[Subjunctive Triggers - Memorize These!]
**NEEDS**:
- il faut que (it's necessary that)
- il est nécessaire que (it's necessary that)

**EMOTIONS**:
- je suis content(e) que (I'm happy that)
- je regrette que (I regret that)
- j'ai peur que (I'm afraid that)

**DOUBT/OPINION**:
- je doute que (I doubt that)
- je ne pense pas que (I don't think that)
- il est possible que (it's possible that)

**DESIRE/WISH**:
- je veux que (I want that)
- je souhaite que (I wish that)
- j'aimerais que (I would like that)
:::

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Mistake #1: Pronunciation Neglect </Highlight>

**Problem**: Learning written French without proper pronunciation
**Solution**: 
- Use Forvo.com (native speaker pronunciations)
- Record yourself, compare with natives
- Practice "r" sound daily (gargle motion!)
- Master nasal vowels early

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Mistake #2: Gender Assumption </Highlight>

**Problem**: Guessing genders instead of learning them
**Solution**:
- ALWAYS learn article with noun (not "maison" but "la maison")
- Use color coding (blue/pink)
- Create mental images (masculine objects in blue, feminine in pink)
- Accept exceptions gracefully!

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Mistake #3: Over-translating </Highlight>

**Problem**: Translating word-for-word from English
**Solution**:
- Accept French logic (J'ai 25 ans = I have 25 years)
- Learn French expressions as units
- Don't ask "why?" - just accept and practice
- Think in French, not English

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Mistake #4: Passive Learning Only </Highlight>

**Problem**: Only reading/listening, never producing
**Solution**:
- Speak from Day 1 (even if alone!)
- Write daily (diary, social media posts)
- Use shadowing technique (repeat after native speakers)
- Active recall > passive recognition
