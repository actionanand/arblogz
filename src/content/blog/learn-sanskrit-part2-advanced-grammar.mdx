## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The 10 Verb Classes (दशगणाः) </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Understanding Gaṇa System </Highlight>

Panini classified ALL Sanskrit verbs into **10 classes (गण - gaṇa)** based on how they form the present tense stem. Each class follows specific patterns.

**The 10 Classes:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Class | Name | Stem Formation | Example Root | Pattern |
|-------|------|----------------|--------------|---------|
| 1 | भ्वादिः (Bhvaadi) | Root + अ (a) | भू → भव (bhava) | Most common |
| 2 | अदादिः (Adaadi) | Root (no change) | अद् → अद् (ad) | Strong root |
| 3 | जुहोत्यादिः (Juhotyaadi) | Root reduplication | हु → जुहो (juho) | Doubled |
| 4 | दिवादिः (Divaadi) | Root + य (ya) | दिव् → दीव्य (deevya) | -ya suffix |
| 5 | स्वादिः (Svaadi) | Root + नु (nu) | सु → सुनु (sunu) | -nu suffix |
| 6 | तुदादिः (Tudaadi) | Root + अ (a) | तुद् → तुद (tuda) | Like Class 1 |
| 7 | रुधादिः (Rudhaadi) | Root + ना/न (naa/na) | रुध् → रुणध् (ruṇadh) | Nasal infix |
| 8 | तनादिः (Tanaadi) | Root + उ (u) | तन् → तनु (tanu) | -u suffix |
| 9 | क्र्यादिः (Kryaadi) | Root + ना/नी (naa/nee) | क्री → क्रीणा (kreeṇaa) | Strong nasal |
| 10 | चुरादिः (Churaadi) | Root + अय (aya) | चुर् → चोरय (choraya) | Causative-like |

</div>

:::note[Tamil Speakers - Comparison]
Sanskrit verb classes are like Tamil verb classifications:
- **Class 1 (भ्वादि)** = Regular Tamil verbs: செய் → செய்கிறேன்
- **Class 10 (चुरादि)** = Causative Tamil: செய்விக்கிறேன் (make someone do)

The stem changes are systematic like Tamil tense markers!
:::

**Why 10 Classes? - ஏன் 10 வகுப்புகள்?**

Panini discovered that Sanskrit verbs follow **10 distinct patterns** for present tense formation. Think of it like:
- **Tamil**: Different verb classes (செய், படி், போ-, வா- etc.)
- **English**: Regular vs irregular verbs (walk-walked vs go-went)

But Sanskrit is MORE systematic - every verb fits into exactly ONE class!

**Most Important Classes to Master:**
1. **Class 1 (भ्वादि)**: 1000+ verbs - MOST common
2. **Class 6 (तुदादि)**: 200+ verbs - very common
3. **Class 4 (दिवादि)**: 100+ verbs - frequent
4. **Class 10 (चुरादि)**: All causatives
5. **Class 2 (अदादि)**: Essential verbs like अस् (to be)

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Complete Tense System - दशलकाराः </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The 10 Lakaras (Tenses/Moods) </Highlight>

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Lakara | Name | Meaning | Usage |
|--------|------|---------|-------|
| लट् (laṭ) | Present | is doing | General present |
| लिट् (liṭ) | Perfect | has done | Recent/witnessed past |
| लुट् (luṭ) | Periphrastic Future | will do (distant) | Future intention |
| लृट् (lṛiṭ) | Simple Future | will do | Simple future |
| लेट् (leṭ) | Vedic Subjunctive | may do | Rare, Vedic only |
| लोट् (loṭ) | Imperative | do! | Commands |
| लङ् (laṅ) | Imperfect | was doing | Past continuous |
| लिङ् (liṅ) | Optative/Potential | may/might do | Possibility/wish |
| लुङ् (luṅ) | Aorist | did (suddenly) | Immediate past |
| लृङ् (lṛiṅ) | Conditional | would do | Hypothetical |

</div>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> लिङ् लकारः - Optative (Wish/Potential) </Highlight>

Used for wishes, polite requests, possibilities, and suggestions.

**पठ् (paṭh - to read) - Optative:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 1st | पठेयम् (paṭheyam) | पठेव (paṭheva) | पठेम (paṭhema) |
| 2nd | पठेः (paṭheḥ) | पठेतम् (paṭhetam) | पठेत (paṭheta) |
| 3rd | पठेत् (paṭhet) | पठेताम् (paṭhetaam) | पठेयुः (paṭheyuḥ) |

</div>

**Examples:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Sanskrit | Tamil | English |
|----------|-------|---------|
| भवान् पठेत् (bhavaan paṭhet) | நீங்கள் படிக்கலாம் | You may/should read |
| अहं गच्छेयम् (aham gachchheyam) | நான் செல்லலாம் | I may go |
| सः श्वः आगच्छेत् (saḥ shvaḥ aagachchhet) | அவன் நாளை வரலாம் | He might come tomorrow |

</div>
:::tip[Optative in Daily Use - தினசரி பயன்பாடு]
**Common Patterns:**

1. **Polite Requests** (அன்பான வேண்டுகோள்):
   - भवान् उपविशेत् (bhavaan upvishet) = Please sit (lit: You may sit)
   - कृपया मम साहाय्यं कुर्यात् (kripayaa mama saahayyam kuryaat) = Kindly help me

2. **Wishes/Blessings** (வாழ்த்துகள்):
   - चिरं जीवेः (chiram jeeveḥ) = May you live long!
   - भवान् सुखी भवेत् (bhavaan sukhee bhavet) = May you be happy!

3. **Suggestions** (பரிந்துரைகள்):
   - वयं गच्छेम (vayam gachchhema) = We might go / Let's go
   - त्वं विश्रामं कुर्याः (tvam vishraamam kuryaaḥ) = You should take rest

4. **Possibilities** (சாத்தியங்கள்):
   - सः आगच्छेत् (saḥ aagachchhet) = He might come
   - वृष्टिः भवेत् (vṛiṣhṭiḥ bhavet) = It may rain
:::
### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> लृङ् लकारः - Conditional (Would/Should) </Highlight>

Expresses hypothetical situations, conditions not met.

**Pattern**: अ + root stem + इष्य + endings

**गम् (gam - to go) - Conditional:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 1st | अगमिष्यम् (agamiṣyam) | अगमिष्याव (agamiṣyaava) | अगमिष्याम (agamiṣyaama) |
| 2nd | अगमिष्यः (agamiṣyaḥ) | अगमिष्यतम् (agamiṣyatam) | अगमिष्यत (agamiṣyata) |
| 3rd | अगमिष्यत् (agamiṣyat) | अगमिष्यताम् (agamiṣyataam) | अगमिष्यन् (agamiṣyan) |

</div>

**Usage Example:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Sanskrit | Tamil | English |
|----------|-------|---------|
| यदि धनं आसीत् तर्हि अहं गृहं क्रेष्यम् (yadi dhanam aaseet tarhi aham griham kreṣyam) | பணம் இருந்தால் நான் வீடு வாங்குவேன் | If (there was) money, I would buy a house |

</div>

**Word-by-word breakdown:**
- यदि (yadi) = if
- धनम् (dhanam) = money/wealth
- आसीत् (aaseet) = was (imperfect of अस्)
- तर्हि (tarhi) = then
- अहम् (aham) = I
- गृहम् (griham) = house
- क्रेष्यम् (kreṣyam) = would buy (conditional of क्री)

:::tip[Conditional Usage - நிபந்தனை பயன்பாடு]
Conditional is used for:
- **Unfulfilled conditions**: Things that didn't happen
- **Hypothetical situations**: Imaginary scenarios
- **Regrets**: "If only I had..."

**Pattern**: यदि (if) + imperfect verb + तर्हि (then) + conditional verb
:::

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Passive Voice - कर्मणिप्रयोगः </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Formation and Usage </Highlight>

In passive voice, the **object becomes subject** (takes nominative case).

**Formation**: Root + **य** (ya) + endings (like Class 4)

**Active vs Passive:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Voice | Sanskrit | Tamil | English |
|-------|----------|-------|---------|
| **Active** | रामः पुस्तकं पठति (raamaḥ pustakam paṭhati) | ராமன் புத்தகம் படிக்கிறான் | Rama reads a book |
| **Passive** | रामेण पुस्तकं पठ्यते (raameṇa pustakam paṭhyate) | ராமனால் புத்தகம் படிக்கப்படுகிறது | A book is read by Rama |

</div>

**Passive Conjugation - पठ् (paṭh):**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 1st | पठ्ये (paṭhye) | पठ्यावहे (paṭhyaavahe) | पठ्यामहे (paṭhyaamahe) |
| 2nd | पठ्यसे (paṭhyase) | पठ्येथे (paṭhyethe) | पठ्यध्वे (paṭhyadhve) |
| 3rd | पठ्यते (paṭhyate) | पठ्येते (paṭhyete) | पठ्यन्ते (paṭhyante) |

</div>

**Key Points:**
- Agent (doer) uses **instrumental case** (तृतीया): रामेण (by Rama)
- Object becomes subject (nominative): पुस्तकम्
- Verb agrees with the object in number

:::tip[When to Use Passive? - செயப்படுபொருள் எப்போது?]
**Use passive when:**
1. **Agent unknown**: पुस्तकं पठ्यते (A book is read - by whom? unknown)
2. **Agent unimportant**: Sanskrit often used in philosophical texts
3. **Focus on action/object**: Emphasizing what happens to the object
4. **Formal/literary style**: Classical texts prefer passive

**Active → Passive Transformation Steps:**
1. Object (accusative) → Subject (nominative)
2. Subject → Instrumental case (or omit)
3. Verb root + य + middle voice endings
4. Verb agrees with NEW subject (the object)

Tamil: செய் → செய்யப்படு (similar passive pattern!)
:::

**More Examples:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Sanskrit | Tamil | English |
|----------|-------|---------|
| सीतया फलं खाद्यते (seetayaa phalam khaadyate) | சீதையால் பழம் சாப்பிடப்படுகிறது | Fruit is eaten by Sita |
| छात्रैः पुस्तकानि पठ्यन्ते (chhatraiḥ pustakaani paṭhyante) | மாணவர்களால் புத்தகங்கள் படிக்கப்படுகின்றன | Books are read by students |

</div>

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Causative Voice - णिजन्तप्रयोगः </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Making Someone Do Something </Highlight>

Causative = making/having someone perform an action.

**Formation**: Root + **अय/आपय** (aya/aapaya)

**Simple → Causative Transformation:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Root | Simple (3rd Sg) | Causative (3rd Sg) | Meaning |
|------|-----------------|---------------------|---------|
| पठ् (paṭh) | पठति (paṭhati) - reads | पाठयति (paaṭhayati) | causes to read/teaches |
| गम् (gam) | गच्छति (gachchhati) - goes | गमयति (gamayati) | causes to go/sends |
| खाद् (khaad) | खादति (khaadati) - eats | खादयति (khaadayati) | feeds |
| पा (paa) | पिबति (pibati) - drinks | पाययति (paayayati) | gives to drink |
| कृ (kṛi) | करोति (karoti) - does | कारयति (kaarayati) | causes to do |

</div>

**Tamil Comparison:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Sanskrit | Romanization | Tamil Equivalent |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| पठति | paṭhati (reads) | படிக்கிறான் |
| पाठयति | paaṭhayati (teaches) | படிக்க வைக்கிறான் / கற்பிக்கிறான் |
| गच्छति | gachchhati (goes) | செல்கிறான் |
| गमयति | gamayati (sends) | அனுப்புகிறான் |
| खादति | khaadati (eats) | சாப்பிடுகிறான் |
| खादयति | khaadayati (feeds) | உணவு ஊட்டுகிறான் |
| पिबति | pibati (drinks) | குடிக்கிறான் |
| पाययति | paayayati (gives to drink) | குடிக்க வைக்கிறான் |

</div>

:::note[Causative Formation Rules - காரண வடிவ விதிகள்]
**Pattern Recognition:**
1. **अ-ending roots**: गम् → गमय (add -अय)
2. **Strong vowels**: कृ → कारय (strengthen vowel + अय)
3. **Vowel change**: पठ् → पाठय (अ → आ + ठ + अय)

**Meaning Shift:**
- Simple verb = subject does action
- Causative = subject makes someone else do action

Like Tamil: செய் (do) → செய்வி (make do)
:::

**Sentence Examples:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Sanskrit | Tamil | English |
|----------|-------|---------|
| शिक्षकः छात्रं पाठयति (shikṣakaḥ chhaatram paaṭhayati) | ஆசிரியர் மாணவனைக் கற்பிக்கிறார் | Teacher teaches the student (makes read) |
| माता बालकं भोजनं खादयति (maataa baalakam bhojanam khaadayati) | தாய் குழந்தைக்கு உணவு ஊட்டுகிறாள் | Mother feeds the child |

</div>

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Compound Words - समासः </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> The 6 Types of Samaasa </Highlight>

Sanskrit creates complex words by combining simpler ones. These are called **समास (samaasa)**.

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Type | Name | Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|---------|---------|
| 1 | अव्ययीभावः (Avyayeebhaava) | Indeclinable first | यथाशक्ति (yathaashakti) | according to ability |
| 2 | तत्पुरुषः (Tatpurusha) | Dependent-Head | राजपुत्रः (raajaputraḥ) | king's son |
| 3 | कर्मधारयः (Karmadhaaraya) | Adjective-Noun | नीलोत्पलम् (neelotpalam) | blue lotus |
| 4 | द्विगुः (Dvigu) | Numeral compound | त्रिलोकी (trilokee) | three worlds |
| 5 | द्वन्द्वः (Dvandva) | Copulative (and) | रामकृष्णौ (raamakṛiṣṇau) | Rama and Krishna |
| 6 | बहुव्रीहिः (Bahuvrīhi) | Possessive | चक्रपाणिः (chakrapaaniḥ) | disk in hand (Vishnu) |

</div>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> तत्पुरुष समासः (Most Common) </Highlight>

Head word is last, first word modifies it (shows relationship via implicit case).

**Understanding Tatpurusha - தத்புருஷ புரிதல்:**

This is THE most important compound type! It's like Tamil's:
- அரசனின் மகன் → அரசமகன் (king's son)
- நாட்டின் பற்று → நாட்டுப்பற்று (patriotism)

**Formation Rule:**
1. Take two words with a case relationship
2. Remove the case ending from first word
3. Combine both words
4. Apply sandhi rules

**Example Process:**
रामस्य पुत्रः (Rama's son - genitive)
→ राम + पुत्रः
→ रामपुत्रः (Ramaputra)

**Examples by Case:**

<div class="table-luxury-grid">

| Compound | Breakdown | Case | Tamil | Meaning |
|----------|-----------|------|-------|---------|
| राजपुत्रः (raajaputraḥ) | राजस्य पुत्रः | षष्ठी (Gen.) | அரசனின் மகன் | king's son |
| देशभक्तिः (deshabhaktiḥ) | देशस्य भक्तिः | षष्ठी | நாட்டுப்பற்று | patriotism (devotion to country) |
| ग्रामगतः (graamagataḥ) | ग्रामं गतः | द्वितीया (Acc.) | கிராமம் சென்றவன் | gone to village |
| शोकविमुक्तः (shokavimuktaḥ) | शोकात् विमुक्तः | पञ्चमी (Abl.) | துக்கத்திலிருந்து விடுபட்டவன் | freed from sorrow |

</div>

## <Highlight color='#800031' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Reading Classical Texts - शास्त्रपाठः </Highlight>

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> Simple Subhashitas (सुभाषितानि) </Highlight>

**Subhashitas** are wise sayings, moral teachings in verse form.

**Example 1:**

```
सत्यं वद । धर्मं चर ।
satyam vada | dharmam chara |
```

**Word-by-word:**
- सत्यम् (satyam) - truth (accusative)
- वद (vada) - speak (imperative)
- धर्मम् (dharmam) - righteousness (accusative)  
- चर (chara) - practice (imperative)

**Tamil**: உண்மை பேசு | தர்மத்தைப் பின்பற்று
**English**: Speak truth | Practice righteousness

---

**Example 2:**

```
विद्या विनयेन शोभते ।
vidyaa vinayena shobhate |
```

**Analysis:**
- विद्या (vidyaa) - knowledge (nominative)
- विनयेन (vinayena) - with humility (instrumental)
- शोभते (shobhate) - shines/adorns (3rd sg present)

**Tamil**: கல்வி பணிவினால் விளங்குகிறது
**English**: Knowledge shines with humility

---

### <Highlight color='#004080' highlight='fg' fontWeight='bold'> From Bhagavad Gita - श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता </Highlight>

**Chapter 2, Verse 47 (simplified):**

```
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
karmaṇyevaadhikaaraste maa phaleṣhu kadaachana |
```

**Sandhi breakdown:**
- कर्मणि + एव + अधिकारः + ते = कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते
- मा + फलेषु + कदाचन

**Word-by-word:**
- कर्मणि (karmaṇi) - in action (locative)
- एव (eva) - only/alone
- अधिकारः (adhikaaraḥ) - right/authority
- ते (te) - your (genitive)
- मा (maa) - not
- फलेषु (phaleṣhu) - in fruits/results (locative)
- कदाचन (kadaachana) - ever

**Tamil**: செயலில் மட்டுமே உனக்கு உரிமை உண்டு, பலனில் ஒருபோதும் இல்லை
**English**: You have right to action alone, never to its results

**Philosophical Context:**
This is one of the most famous verses in Hinduism! It teaches **Karma Yoga** - doing your duty without attachment to results.

---

**Example 2 - From Hitopadesha:**

```
विद्या ददाति विनयं विनयाद् याति पात्रताम् ।
पात्रत्वात् धनमाप्नोति धनात् धर्मं ततः सुखम् ॥
```

**Transliteration:**
vidyaa dadaati vinayam vinayaad yaati paatrataam |
paatratvaad dhanamapnoti dhanaat dharmam tataḥ sukham ||

**Word-by-word:**
- विद्या (vidyaa) - knowledge
- ददाति (dadaati) - gives
- विनयम् (vinayam) - humility
- विनयात् (vinayaat) - from humility (ablative)
- याति (yaati) - goes/attains
- पात्रताम् (paatrataam) - worthiness
- पात्रत्वात् (paatrattvaat) - from worthiness
- धनम् (dhanam) - wealth
- आप्नोति (aapnoti) - obtains
- धर्मम् (dharmam) - righteousness
- ततः (tataḥ) - from that
- सुखम् (sukham) - happiness

**Tamil**: கல்வி பணிவைக் கொடுக்கிறது, பணிவிலிருந்து தகுதி வருகிறது, தகுதியிலிருந்து செல்வம் கிடைக்கிறது, செல்வத்திலிருந்து தர்மம், அதிலிருந்து இன்பம்.

**English**: Knowledge gives humility, from humility comes worthiness, from worthiness one gets wealth, from wealth righteousness, and from that happiness.

**Grammar Points:**
- Uses ablative case (पञ्चमी): विनयात्, पात्रत्वात्, धनात् - showing "from"
- Chain of cause-effect relationships
- Beautiful logical progression
