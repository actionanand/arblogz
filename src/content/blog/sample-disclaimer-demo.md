## Testing the Disclaimer Feature

This is a sample blog post that demonstrates the disclaimer feature.

### What You'll See

Below the content (and after any related articles), you'll see an attractive disclaimer section with:

- 🔔 An alert icon
- 📝 The default disclaimer text (automatically translated to your site language)
- 🎨 Styled with your site's accent color

### Try Custom Disclaimer

To use a custom disclaimer instead of the default, add this to your frontmatter:

```yaml
showDisclaimer: true
customDisclaimer: "Your custom disclaimer text here"
```

### When to Use

Use disclaimers for:
- Health and medical content
- Legal information
- Financial advice
- Technical tutorials
- Product reviews
- DIY guides

Enjoy the new feature!
